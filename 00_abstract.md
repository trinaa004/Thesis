## ABSTRACT

The rapid evolution of the Meetings, Incentives, Conventions, and Exhibitions (MICE) industry and regional civic event spaces demands adaptable venues capable of executing swift layout turnarounds while expressing local identity. Officially designated by the City Government of Calbayog as a vital hub for culture, arts, tourism, and community gatherings, the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center (locally referenced as the Calbayog City Convention Center) operates within a rigid, unzoned interior envelope that causes prolonged setup times and spatial inefficiencies. This study aims to formulate a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework and layout manual for the venue’s 840 square meter interior envelope, comprising the 711 square meter Main Convention Hall and 129 square meter Lobby area. Adopting an Applied Design Research approach, the study employs a convergent parallel mixed-methods design. Quantitative data will be gathered from 115 to 145 respondents (15–25 event planners via expert sampling and 100–120 attendees via Slovin's formula quota sampling) concurrently with qualitative interviews with 8 to 12 key informants (including venue managers, event organizers, and cultural officers) and interior site mapping. Survey data will be analyzed using descriptive statistics, interview transcripts using Braun and Clarke’s (2006) thematic analysis, and findings triangulated with anthropometric (Panero & Zelnik; Neufert) and accessibility standards (BP 344; ADA). The resulting framework integrates modular furniture typologies (Plenary/Theatrical, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition/Gallery), an adaptable overhead ceiling rigging interface, and non-structural patterned surface treatments abstracting Samar Basey *banig* weaving, *tikog* grass textures, Calbayog waterfall fluid forms, and Hadang festival cultural movements to establish an actionable interior design guide that substantiates operational viability, spatial efficiency, and regional civic identity.

**Keywords**: *Interior Design Framework, Modular Space Planning, Calbayog Cultural Convention Center, Local Samar Heritage, Hadang Festival, MICE Industry, Overhead Ceiling Rigging Systems, Universal Accessibility.*
