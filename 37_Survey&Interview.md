# PART 1: INFORMED CONSENT FORM

**Dear Participant,**

You are invited to participate in a research study aimed at formulating a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework for the 840 square meter Main Hall of the Calbayog City Convention Center. This study explores how modular furniture typologies, adaptable overhead ceiling track/rigging interfaces, localized Samar cultural material treatments (*banig* mat weaving, *tikog* grass, cascading waterfall motifs), and future-proof design principles can enhance spatial efficiency, user experience, and operational turnaround speed.

Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to answer any specific question or withdraw your consent at any time without penalty or loss of benefits.  

Depending on your stakeholder profile, you will be asked to complete a structured survey questionnaire (taking approximately 10 to 15 minutes) or participate in an audio-recorded semi-structured interview (taking approximately 20 to 30 minutes).  

In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), all data collected will remain strictly confidential. No personally identifiable information will be published; all data will be aggregated and assigned alphanumeric codes (e.g., Respondent P-01, Respondent A-05). All electronic data will be stored in password-protected, encrypted files accessible exclusively to the researcher and will be permanently deleted upon completion of the study.  

There are no foreseeable physical or psychological risks associated with this study. While there is no direct monetary compensation, your valuable insights will directly contribute to elevating the operational adaptability, community accessibility, and regional cultural identity of civic infrastructure in Calbayog City, Samar.

# PART 4: QUALITATIVE SEMI-STRUCTURED INTERVIEW PROTOCOL FOR EVENT PLANNERS & VENUE ADMINISTRATORS

**Target Informants**: Selected Event Planners, Coordinators, Venue Managers, and Technical Directors ($n = 8 \text{ to } 12$).  
**Format**: 20-to-30-minute semi-structured, audio-recorded qualitative interview session.  

---

### INTERVIEW GUIDE QUESTIONS

#### Module 1: Existing Operational & Setup Bottlenecks (SPOP Q1)
1. What are the primary spatial and physical challenges you encounter when setting up or tearing down events in the 840 sqm Main Hall of the Calbayog City Convention Center?
2. How long does a typical venue setup take for your events, and what specific furniture or interior limitations cause turnaround delays?
3. How do current acoustic reverberation and ambient lighting conditions affect event execution?

#### Module 2: Spatial Modularity & Furniture Flexibility (SPOP Q2 & Q5)
4. What types of modular furniture (FF&E), mobile partitions, or convertible staging systems would most dramatically improve your setup speed and spatial efficiency?
5. How would having standardized layout templates (e.g., Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition) change your space planning workflow?
6. What anthropometric clearance issues (e.g., aisle widths, server passage, seat spacing) do you frequently observe during large banquets or exhibitions?

#### Module 3: Overhead Ceiling Track & Rigging Systems (SPOP Q4)
7. How could an adjustable overhead ceiling track and rigging system benefit your lighting, sound, or decorative drapery setups?
8. In what ways would motorized overhead truss hoists improve safety and floor space utilization during major events?

#### Module 4: Cultural Heritage Expression & Sense of Place (SPOP Q3 & Q6)
9. How do you feel about integrating traditional Samar Basey *banig* mat weaving patterns, *tikog* grass textures, and Calbayog waterfall imagery into permanent wall, floor, and ceiling finishes?
10. How can we balance strong cultural expression with high spatial reconfigurability so that permanent motifs do not restrict room versatility?
11. What long-term future-proof interior features or sustainable materials do you believe are essential for keeping the Calbayog City Convention Center competitive over the next 10 to 15 years?
