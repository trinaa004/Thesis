Data Collection
Data collection will be executed in a systematic, phased manner following institutional research protocols:
Permissions & Ethical Clearance: Official written permission to conduct the study will be secured from the Calbayog City Convention Center management and local municipal authorities. Informed consent forms will be provided to all participants prior to data collection.
Pilot Testing & Instrument Reliability: Because the survey questionnaire is a researcher-developed instrument, a pilot test will be administered to 10 individuals (comprising event coordinators and frequent venue attendees excluded from the final study sample). This pre-test evaluates item readability, contextual clarity, structural flow, and internal consistency (Cronbach's alpha). Necessary adjustments to item phrasing and scale layout will be implemented based on pilot feedback prior to final survey deployment.
Survey Administration: The survey questionnaire will be administered through both printed self-administered copies and digital survey forms (e.g., Google Forms) to accommodate participants' schedules. Clear instructions will be provided, and completed surveys will be securely collected and encoded.
Conducting Semi-Structured Interviews: Interviews will be scheduled at mutually agreed times, either in person or via digital teleconferencing platforms. Each session will last approximately 20 to 30 minutes. With explicit participant consent, interviews will be audio-recorded and subsequently transcribed verbatim.
On-Site Spatial Observation: Field observations and architectural mapping of the 840 sqm Main Hall will be scheduled during both active event setups (to observe operational logistics) and non-event periods (to take accurate spatial measurements and photograph interior envelope details).
