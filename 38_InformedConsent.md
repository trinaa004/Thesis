### 2. Purpose of the Research / Katuyu-an san Pag-aram
The purpose of this study is to formulate a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework for the 840 square meter Main Hall of the Calbayog City Convention Center in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. The study addresses existing operational bottlenecks, setup turnaround delays, acoustic reverberation, and circulation constraints while integrating regional Samar heritage (Basey *banig* weaving patterns, *tikog* grass textures, Waraynon motifs) and Calbayog waterfall imagery into an adaptable, future-proof interior space.  
*Waraynon: An katuyu-an san ini nga pag-aram kay an paghimo san usa nga non-structural interior design framework para ha 840 square meter nga Main Hall san Calbayog City Convention Center ha Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. Ini nga pag-aram kay nagtatadong san mga problema ha interior, pagkaiha san pag-setup, lanog (echo), ug agi-an san tawo, habang nag-uupod san kultura san Samar (disenyo san banig nga Basey, tekstura san tikog, Waray geometric motifs) ug busay san Calbayog para ha usa nga adaptable ug future-proof nga lugar.*
