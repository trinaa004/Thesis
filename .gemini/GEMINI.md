## Rationale Planning & Editing Rule

When planning, drafting, or making changes to the thesis rationale (`01_rationale.md`), ALWAYS reference and align with the following core documents:

1. **`00_introduction.md`** – Introduction and overall project background.
2. **`02_chapter1.md`** – Chapter 1 framework, objectives, problem statements, and scope.
3. **`20_RRL.md`** – Review of Related Literature (RRL), theoretical framework, ergonomic standards, and cultural design references.

### Core Instructions:
* Before proposing or executing any modifications to `01_rationale.md`, cross-check against `00_introduction.md`, `02_chapter1.md`, and `20_RRL.md`.
* Ensure venue specifications (Calbayog City Convention Center, 840 sqm Main Hall), key concepts (MICE industry, modularity, adaptability, future-proof design, motorized truss hoists), and cultural references (Samar *banig* weaving, *tikog* grass) remain strictly consistent across all files.
