# Rationale of the Study

As the event industry demands evolve, so does the need for spaces that can cater to multiple functions efficiently and seamlessly (Ike et al., 2023). The Calbayog City Convention Center primarily functions as a multi-purpose venue rather than a facility solely dedicated to static meetings and conferences, hosting diverse events such as trade fairs, social gatherings, exhibitions, and cultural performances. However, the 840 square meter Main Hall currently lacks spatial flexibility, modularity, and adaptable interior infrastructure. This limits its capacity to swiftly transition between contrasting event setups. Because event centers serve as vital gathering hubs for diverse audiences (Ayuba & Agah, 2018), there is an urgent need for an adaptable interior framework within the venue's interior envelope.

---

Calbayog City is actively pursuing eco-tourism as a key driver of municipal development and regional sustainability. However, as Roa and Lagamon (2018) noted, there are growing concerns regarding the preservation of Samar’s historical and cultural resources, where inadequate preservation measures have threatened local heritage assets. Compounding this challenge is the diminishing public awareness among locals regarding their cultural identity. In this context, culturally responsive interior design becomes essential. By grounding interior surfaces in Samar’s rich Basey banig mat weaving heritage, interior design serves as an active, experiential medium for cultural preservation, education, and civic pride.

---

Future-proof design forms another critical pillar of this study. The evolving nature of MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) venues necessitates interior environments that continuously adapt to technological advancements, shifting event trends, and operational demands. Implementing reconfigurable furniture, fixtures, and equipment (FF&E) alongside modular spatial layouts ensures long-term functionality and sustainability without requiring costly structural overhauls. Furthermore, utilizing the overhead ceiling plane to house flexible track systems and motorized truss hoists enables effortless adjustments to ambient lighting, acoustic drapes, and spatial dividers.

---

This study is driven by the necessity to elevate the operational adaptability, regional identity, and long-term usability of the Calbayog City Convention Center. Contemporary event venues increasingly incorporate modular interior zoning and customizable spatial configurations to maximize functional efficiency (Ike et al., 2023). Crucially, while the convention center must remain a versatile, rentable space, integrating localized Samar Basey banig motifs into permanent and semi-permanent surface treatments establishes a distinctive sense of place without hindering layout flexibility (Fakharany, 2025).

---

Ultimately, this study aims to formulate a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework consisting of modular interior typologies, ergonomic layout standards, and overhead ceiling interfaces for the 840 square meter Main Hall. By harmonizing flexible spatial configurations with Samar’s identity and sustainable material specifications, the proposed framework enhances spatial efficiency, optimizes setup turnarounds for venue operators, and reinforces community connection for long-term civic utility.
