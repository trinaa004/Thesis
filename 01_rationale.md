 Rationale of the Study

As the event industry demands evolve, so does the need for spaces that can cater to multiple functions efficiently and seamlessly (Ike et al., 2023). Officially designated by the City Government of Calbayog as the **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center** (locally referenced as the Calbayog City Convention Center), the facility is chartered as a vital civic hub for promoting local culture, arts, tourism, and community assemblies (City Government of Calbayog, n.d.). Rather than functioning solely as a static meeting hall, the venue serves as a versatile, rentable space hosting diverse programs ranging from government summits, educational conferences, and trade fairs to social gatherings and cultural performances. However, the venue's 840 square meter interior envelope, comprising the 711 square meter Main Hall and 129 square meter Lobby area, currently lacks spatial flexibility, modular interior zoning, and adaptable interior infrastructure. This limits its capacity to swiftly transition between contrasting event setups. Because event centers serve as vital gathering hubs for diverse audiences (Ayuba & Agah, 2018), there is an urgent need for an adaptable, site-specific interior framework within the venue's interior envelope.

---

Beyond spatial layout rigidity, the venue's existing indoor environmental quality (IEQ) presents critical operational and experiential deficiencies. As Frontczak and Wargocki (2011) and Chojnacki et al. (2023) emphasize, indoor environmental parameters, specifically acoustic intelligibility, ambient lighting control, and thermal comfort, directly dictate occupant satisfaction and functional performance in multi-use assembly spaces. In its current state, the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center's Main Hall features hard ceramic tile flooring, reflective perimeter glass walls lined with aging drapery, and an unbaffled ceiling without any acoustic absorption, resulting in acoustic reverberation, flutter echoes, and compromised speech clarity during large assemblies without much decoration. Furthermore, the absence of zoned ambient lighting control and balanced thermal distribution detracts from the attendee experience. Addressing these baseline IEQ challenges through non-structural acoustic overlays and adaptive lighting interfaces is essential to elevating the facility into a high-performance civic venue.

---

Calbayog City is widely celebrated as the center for culture and the arts in Eastern Visayas, actively pursuing eco-tourism and cultural preservation as key drivers of municipal development and regional identity. Samar possesses a profound material craft heritage through Basey *banig* weaving, as well as renowned living cultural traditions such as the **Hadang Festival (historically and popularly recognized as the Sarakiki-Hadang Festival)**. While dedicated municipal spaces such as the **Handumanan Museum** host art exhibits, and the high-capacity **Calbayog City Sports Center** accommodates mass-spectator festival showdowns, the Cultural Convention Center represents an essential, affordable indoor venue for civic assemblies, institutional symposiums, and curated cultural gatherings (such as the landmark Samar-wide *Artists Kolektib* assembly). However, as Roa and Lagamon (2018) noted, inadequate preservation measures and generic public interiors have often failed to celebrate local heritage assets. Understanding how the convention center's spatial and interior conditions influence its booking utilization requires empirical inquiry with event organizers, the City Arts and Culture Office (CACO), and facility operators. In this context, culturally responsive interior design becomes essential: by grounding interior surfaces in Samar’s rich Basey *banig* mat weaving geometry, *tikog* grass textures, Calbayog’s celebrated waterfalls, and the dynamic, ritualistic movements of the Hadang Festival, interior design serves as an active, experiential medium for cultural preservation, education, and civic pride without restricting layout versatility (Krstić et al., 2024).

---

Future-proof design forms another critical pillar of this study. The evolving nature of MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) venues necessitates interior environments that continuously adapt to technological advancements, shifting event trends, and operational demands (DesignHorizons, 2024; Dong et al., 2024). Implementing reconfigurable furniture, fixtures, and equipment (FF&E) alongside modular spatial layouts ensures long-term functionality and sustainability without requiring costly structural overhauls. Furthermore, utilizing the overhead ceiling plane to house flexible track systems and motorized truss hoists enables effortless adjustments to ambient lighting, acoustic drapes, and spatial dividers.

---

While flagship convention centers like the Philippine International Convention Center (PICC) illustrate the macroeconomic value of the MICE industry, provincial venues operate under distinct spatial, budgetary, and infrastructural constraints. The successful rehabilitation and interior retrofitting of the **Baguio Convention and Cultural Center (BCCC)** demonstrates that aging municipal facilities chartered for both convention and cultural mandates can achieve world-class MICE functionality, universal accessibility, and acoustic excellence through targeted, non-structural interior interventions (City Government of Baguio, 2019; DPWH, 2021). By adopting a similar interior-focused framework, the 840 square meter interior envelope (711 sqm Main Hall and 129 sqm Lobby) of the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center can overcome its rigid spatial configuration and turnaround bottlenecks, transitioning from an underutilized municipal hall into an adaptable, culturally resonant civic landmark for Western Samar.

---

This study is driven by the necessity to elevate the spatial modularity, regional identity, and long-term usability of the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center. Contemporary event venues increasingly incorporate modular interior zoning and customizable spatial configurations to maximize functional efficiency (Ike et al., 2023). Crucially, while the convention center must remain a versatile, rentable space, integrating localized Samar Basey *banig* motifs alongside fluid, cascading references to Calbayog as the "City of Waterfalls" and dynamic Hadang cultural movements into permanent and semi-permanent surface treatments establishes a distinctive sense of place without hindering layout flexibility (Fakharany, 2025).

---

Ultimately, this study aims to formulate evidence-based interior design interventions consisting of repeatable modular layout templates, ergonomic circulation standards, and overhead ceiling interfaces for the 840 sqm interior envelope (711 sqm Main Hall & 129 sqm Lobby). By harmonizing flexible spatial configurations with Samar and Calbayog's cultural identity and sustainable material specifications, the proposed framework enhances spatial efficiency, resolves operational turnaround bottlenecks for event planners and custodial staff, and reinforces community place attachment for long-term civic utility.
