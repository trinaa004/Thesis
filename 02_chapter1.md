# Chapter 1: Research Framework

## Statement of the Problem
This study aims to investigate how the integration of modular, culturally-responsive, and future-proof interior design strategies can improve the flexibility, spatial efficiency, and cultural identity of the Calbayog City Convention Center’s interior. In doing so, this study seeks to answer the following key questions:

1. What are the current spatial and operational challenges within the Calbayog City Convention Center’s interior envelope that hinder user experience and layout efficiency? 
2. How can modular interior zoning and flexible FF&E (Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment) configurations be applied to accommodate diverse event formats seamlessly? 
3. In what ways can localized Samar heritage motifs and cultural elements be woven into interior surface treatments to cultivate a distinct sense of place without restricting space versatility? 
4. How can the overhead ceiling plane be utilized to integrate adjustable spatial mechanisms and localized ambient controls for event setup efficiency? 
5. What programmatic parameters are needed to establish an adaptable array of interior configurations while leaving room for user customization? 
6. What interior material and technological specifications can future-proof the space against evolving functional demands? 

---

## Research Objectives
This study aims to develop a site-specific interior design framework consisting of modular interior typologies and layout standards that adapt to the diverse event demands of the Calbayog City Convention Center, which integrates modular, culturally-responsive, and future-proof design principles, to enhance spatial efficiency, user experience, and long-term functionality for both event planners and users of the space. Specifically, it seeks to:

* Evaluate the existing layout configurations, user circulation, and material conditions of the Calbayog City Convention Center's interior spaces. 
* Formulate modular furniture layouts and interior zoning strategies that maximize spatial capacity and configuration speed. 
* Synthesize culturally-responsive design elements, inspired by the traditions of Calbayog and the Samar region, into the space's permanent and semi-permanent interior surfaces. 
* Propose an adaptable overhead system, combining modular acoustic ceiling finishes with a flexible track and rigging framework, to facilitate quick transformations in mood, lighting, and interior draping. 
* Apply future-proof interior specifications regarding smart technologies, acoustics, and sustainable materials to maintain long-term relevance. 

---

## Significance of the Study
The results of the study will benefit the following:

* **Event Planners and Venue Operators:** This study provides a concrete, logistical toolkit by offering pre-calculated layout standards and modular spatial typologies. This directly minimizes event turnaround times, eliminates trial-and-error space planning, and optimizes anthropometric clearances for logistical staff, thereby streamlining operational efficiency. 
* **Event-Goers and Visitors:** This study enhances the immediate interior environment through optimized spatial zoning, intuitive wayfinding, and controlled acoustics. Furthermore, the intentional integration of local Samar cultural design elements creates a deeper psychological connection and an immersive sensory experience for the public. 
* **The Local Government:** By introducing a future-proof interior framework, the municipality is equipped with a high-performance civic venue capable of effortlessly scaling to accommodate diverse institutional, economic, and tourism-driven events, optimizing the long-term utility and economic viability of local public infrastructure. 
* **The Local Community of Calbayog City:** This study will ensure that the convention center will become a culturally relevant and accessible space that benefits local businesses and artists. 
* **Interior Design Professionals and Future Researchers:** This study can be a guide for future studies, offering a basis that they can replicate and explore in greater depth.

## Scope and Limitations of the Study

### Scope of the Study
The scope of this study is strictly confined to the development of a non-structural interior design framework and layout manual for the main interior hall of the Calbayog City Convention Center. Specifically, the study includes: 

* Designing a standardized menu of repeatable interior layout templates (e.g., Plenary, Exhibition, and Banquet configurations) optimized for the specific square footage of the venue. 
* Formulating ergonomic furniture density arrangements and mobile spatial dividers to maximize spatial efficiency and minimize setup/teardown turnaround times for event planners. 
* Integrating localized Samar heritage motifs, material selections, and traditional weaving patterns (such as Samat or Lara weaves) into permanent and semi-permanent interior surface planes (walls, floors, and ceiling finishes) to establish a distinct regional identity. 
* Proposing a non-structural overhead ceiling interface that layers modular acoustic finish tiles with an adjustable, conceptual interior track-and-rigging framework to facilitate dynamic alterations in ambient lighting and decorative drapery. 

The spatial application of this framework is strictly limited to the interior envelope of the Main Convention Hall, encompassing a total floor area of 840 square meters. 

### Limitations of the Study
To protect the professional boundaries of this study and maintain a focused research scope, the study establishes the following explicit limitations: 

* This study is a non-structural interior overlay. It completely excludes modifications to the primary building shell, exterior facades, load-bearing columns, and structural roof trusses. No structural load-bearing calculations or civil engineering revisions will be performed. 
* While the framework conceptually accounts for technological integration, this study will not provide detailed engineering schematics, calculations, or circuit designs for Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing (MEP), HVAC ducting rerouting, acoustic engineering ratings, or commercial rigging hardware systems. These elements are treated purely as spatial and aesthetic design interfaces. 
* The design intervention is strictly internal. All outdoor zones, landscaping, parking facilities, building services, and ancillary interior zones (such as administrative offices, back-of-house catering kitchens, loading docks, and public restrooms) are outside the scope of this study. 
* Field recommendations and spatial parameters are synthesized from empirical survey and interview feedback gathered from event planners and attendees of the Calbayog City Convention Center (adapted from An, Kim, & Hur, 2021), combined with global interior design standards, anthropometric data, and documented industry best practices. 
* The scope is strictly limited to spatial planning and physical interior design strategies. It explicitly excludes venue operational policies, event marketing strategies, financial budgeting, and facility management business models.
