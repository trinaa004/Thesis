Dear **Dr. Ruby S. Suazo** and Members of **Review Panel 2**,

I am writing to formally submit my research proposal, **"An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Local Samar Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability,"** for initial ethics review and clearance by the University of San Carlos Research Ethics Committee (USC REC).

Municipal facilities such as the Calbayog City Convention Center frequently face operational challenges within their 840 square meter interior envelope (711 sqm Main Hall and 129 sqm Foyer), including prolonged setup and teardown turnaround times, service circulation bottlenecks, acoustic reverberation issues, and sterile interior aesthetics. To address these deficiencies, this study formulates a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework. The proposed framework integrates modular overhead utility track/rigging interfaces, standardized furniture, fixtures, and equipment (FF&E) layout typologies (Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition), universal accessibility compliance (Batas Pambansa Bilang 344 and ADA Standards), and a cultural surface pattern matrix abstracting regional Samar *banig* weaving motifs, *tikog* grass textures, and cascading waterfall imagery.

To establish empirical, user-centered baseline parameters for spatial efficiency and cultural design resonance, this study incorporates field data collection involving **115 to 145 human subjects** selected via purposive and convenience sampling. The sample comprises a **specialized expert group** of 15–25 event planners, coordinators, production managers, and facility operators, alongside a **representative public group** of 100–120 frequent venue attendees. Data will be gathered using self-administered printed and digital questionnaires (Google Forms), 20-to-30-minute semi-structured interviews, and non-participatory spatial mapping under voluntary participation and participant safety protocols. In full compliance with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)**, all participants will execute Informed Consent Forms, and all collected data will be anonymized using alphanumeric identifiers, stored on encrypted cloud drives, and permanently purged upon final thesis approval.

I respectfully submit the accompanying REC application documents for your review and evaluation. Thank you very much for your time, consideration, and guidance.
