Research Respondents
To ensure a comprehensive evaluation of both operational venue logistics and overall public attendee experience, data for this study will be gathered from two primary respondent groups possessing direct experience with the Calbayog City Convention Center. The inclusion of these two distinct stakeholder groups ensures that both expert operational requirements and public user needs inform the formulation of the interior design framework.
	The first respondent group consists of professional event planners, event coordinators, venue managers, and logistical operators who have organized, managed, or executed events, such as conferences, trade shows, social galas, exhibitions, or municipal summits, inside the convention center. This group represents the operational stakeholders of the venue. Their domain expertise will be leveraged to evaluate operational logistics, setup and teardown turnaround times, spatial bottlenecks, Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment (FF&E) reconfigurability, and overhead ceiling rigging requirements.
	The second respondent group comprises general community members, local residents, and event-goers who have attended one or more gatherings at the convention center. This group represents the primary public users of the space. Their feedback will capture the attendee user experience, focusing on interior spatial comfort, circulation efficiency, seating sightlines, acoustic quality, intuitive wayfinding, and cultural ambiance.
Selection Criteria:
To guarantee data validity and operational relevance, all participants recruited for this study must satisfy explicit inclusion criteria. Specifically, respondents must:
1. Be at least 18 years of age at the time of data collection.
2. Have organized, managed, facilitated, or attended at least one event inside the Calbayog City Convention Center (Main Hall or Foyer) within the past five years.
3. Possess familiarity with the venue's interior space or the regional cultural context of Samar.

Inclusivity and Physical Accessibility Accommodations:
Participant recruitment actively accommodates and encourages the inclusion of individuals with diverse physical accessibility needs, specifically persons with mobility impairments (such as wheelchair users and individuals utilizing assistive mobility devices). The inclusion of participants with physical mobility limitations is essential to critically evaluate the venue's spatial clearances, corridor widths, ramp transitions, integrated accessible seating stalls, and barrier-free circulation against Batas Pambansa Bilang 344 (BP 344 - Accessibility Law) and universal design criteria. To facilitate unhindered participation, research instruments will be administered in physically accessible venue zones, offered via digital formats (Google Forms), or completed with researcher assistance upon request.


Phase 1: Pre-Data Gathering Coordinative Actions & Permissions
	Prior to initiating field data collection, formal coordinative actions will be executed following clearance from the University of San Carlos Research Ethics Committee (USC REC):
Institutional Permissions & Site Access: Official transmittal and permission letters will be submitted to the Calbayog City Convention Center management and local municipal authorities to secure administrative site authorization, access to venue event logs, and permission for on-site mapping of the 840 square meter interior envelope (711 sqm Main Hall and 129 sqm Foyer).
Expert Participant Outreach: Formal invitation letters detailing the study's scope, voluntary nature, and time commitment will be transmitted directly to identified event planners, venue operators, and production managers for expert survey and interview recruitment.
General Attendee Recruitment: To recruit general event attendees, call-for-participation posters will be displayed on venue bulletin boards during scheduled public events, supplemented by digital call-for-participation announcements posted on verified local community and civic social media pages.
Bilingual Recruitment Materials & Language Inclusivity: To prevent linguistic barriers from excluding local community members, grassroots event attendees, or facility end-users, all recruitment materials—including informational flyers, bulletin posters, and preliminary consent notices—will be made available in both English and Waray (the local vernacular of Calbayog City and Samar).
Ethical Safeguards & Data Privacy: All recruitment materials, invitation letters, and transmittal documents will feature official university credentials, researcher contact information, and explicit notifications regarding voluntary participation, informed consent, and safeguards under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).
Time-Bound Linking Key Destruction & Permanent Anonymization: To eliminate re-identification risk and protect participant confidentiality, the master linking key pairing participant identities with assigned alphanumeric codes will be stored in an isolated, encrypted directory and permanently destroyed via digital file shredding within thirty (30) calendar days following the final thesis defense. This immediately renders the remaining analytical dataset permanently anonymized for the duration of the one-year retention period.
