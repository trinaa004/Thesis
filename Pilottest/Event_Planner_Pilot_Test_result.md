## 1. Executive Summary & Reliability Threshold Benchmark

To evaluate operational setup workflows, turnaround bottlenecks, FF&E reconfigurability, and overhead ceiling track utility prior to main field sampling, a pilot pre-test was administered to 10 qualified event coordinators, venue operators, and event logistics managers. The questionnaire measures five core physical and logistical dimensions of the Calbayog City Convention Center Main Hall across 25 Likert-scale items (1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree).
