## Alternative Dual-Naming Title Variations

1. **Compound Hyphenated Title (Recommended - Sleek & Concise)**:
   > **"An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Samar-Calbayog Cultural Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability"**
   * *Why it works*: Hyphenating *"Samar-Calbayog"* adds zero length clutter while perfectly uniting provincial regional craft (Samar *banig* weaving) with municipal city identity (Calbayog waterfalls).

2. **Explicit Dual-Naming Title**:
   > **"An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Samar and Calbayog Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability"**
   * *Why it works*: Direct, formal, and clear. Explicitly names both Samar and Calbayog without being overly long.

3. **Strategy-First Dual-Naming Title**:
   > **"Harmonizing Spatial Modularity and Samar-Calbayog Heritage: A Non-Structural Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center"**
   * *Why it works*: Flips the title to make it punchy and memorable while keeping both geographic names.

## Defense Script: Answering Panel Questions on Samar vs. Calbayog Heritage

> **Panel Question**: *"Why did you include both Samar and Calbayog in your cultural title? Is there a distinction between Samar heritage and Calbayog heritage in your interior design?"*
> 
> **Prepared Student Response**: 
> *"Thank you for that question. We explicitly included both Samar and Calbayog because our interior design framework synthesizes two distinct levels of cultural identity:*
> 1. * **Regional Samar Heritage**: Supplies the physical material and craft vocabulary—specifically the traditional Basey banig mat weaving patterns, Waray geometric motifs, and natural tikog grass textures native to the Samar region.*
> 2. * **Local Calbayog Identity**: Supplies the site-specific visual imagery—specifically the cascading fluid motifs celebrating Calbayog as the 'City of Waterfalls'.*
> 
> *By combining them into a unified 'Samar-Calbayog Cultural Heritage' framework, the interior envelope pays homage to regional Samar craft while establishing an unmistakable, site-specific sense of place for Calbayog City."*
