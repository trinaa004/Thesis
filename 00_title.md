## Alternative Dual-Naming Title Variations

1. **Compound Hyphenated Title (Recommended - Sleek & Comprehensive)**:
   > **"An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Samar-Calbayog Cultural Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability"**
   * *Why it works*: Reflects the official statutory name designated on the City Government of Calbayog portal while uniting regional provincial craft (Samar *banig* weaving) with municipal identity (Calbayog waterfalls and Hadang cultural traditions).

2. **Explicit Dual-Naming Title**:
   > **"An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Samar and Calbayog Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability"**
   * *Why it works*: Direct, formal, and clear. Explicitly names both Samar and Calbayog without unnecessary length.

3. **Strategy-First Dual-Naming Title**:
   > **"Harmonizing Spatial Modularity and Samar-Calbayog Heritage: A Non-Structural Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center"**
   * *Why it works*: Flips the title to make it punchy and memorable while keeping the official facility name and both geographic identities.

## Defense Scripts: Answering Panel Questions

### 1. Panel Question on Facility Naming: Calbayog Cultural Convention Center
> **Panel Question**: *"Why does your title state 'Calbayog Cultural Convention Center' when locals often call it the 'Calbayog City Convention Center'?"*
> 
> **Prepared Student Response**: 
> *"Thank you for pointing that out. While local residents and informal event bookings commonly refer to the venue as the 'Calbayog City Convention Center' or simply the 'convention center', the facility is officially chartered and documented by the City Government of Calbayog as the **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center**. The official LGU mandate designates it as a vital hub for promoting Calbayog City's culture, arts, tourism, and community assemblies. Utilizing its official institutional name aligns our research directly with the LGU's statutory vision while addressing the physical interior realities of the venue."*

### 2. Panel Question on Samar vs. Calbayog Heritage
> **Panel Question**: *"Why did you include both Samar and Calbayog in your cultural title? Is there a distinction between Samar heritage and Calbayog heritage in your interior design?"*
> 
> **Prepared Student Response**: 
> *"Thank you for that question. We explicitly included both Samar and Calbayog because our interior design framework synthesizes two distinct levels of cultural identity:*
> 1. * **Regional Samar Heritage**: Supplies the physical material and craft vocabulary—specifically the traditional Basey banig mat weaving patterns, Waray geometric motifs, and natural tikog grass textures native to the Samar region.*
> 2. * **Local Calbayog Identity**: Supplies the site-specific visual imagery—specifically the cascading fluid motifs celebrating Calbayog as the 'City of Waterfalls' and the kinetic energy of the local Hadang festival traditions.*
> 
> *By combining them into a unified 'Samar-Calbayog Cultural Heritage' framework, the interior envelope pays homage to regional Samar craft while establishing an unmistakable, site-specific sense of place for Calbayog City."*
