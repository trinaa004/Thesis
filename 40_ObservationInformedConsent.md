### 2. Purpose of the Research / Katuyu-an san Pag-aram
The purpose of this study is to formulate a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework and layout manual for the 840 square meter interior envelope (comprising the 711 square meter Main Hall and 129 square meter Lobby) of the Calbayog City Convention Center in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. The study addresses real-world logistical bottlenecks, prolonged setup and teardown turnaround times, floor cabling hazards, acoustic reverberation, and circulation pinch points. The on-site spatial and event lifecycle observation aims to empirically document actual setup sequences, crowd circulation patterns, and teardown timelines across different event typologies (e.g., Plenary assemblies, Banquets, and Exhibitions) to establish an authentic baseline for modular interior layout standards.  
*Waraynon: An katuyu-an san ini nga pag-aram kay an paghimo san usa nga non-structural interior design framework ug layout manual para ha 840 square meter nga interior envelope (711 sqm Main Hall ug 129 sqm Lobby) san Calbayog City Convention Center ha Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. Ini nga pag-aram kay nagtatadong san mga problema ha logistics, pagkaiha san setup ug teardown turnaround, mga peligro ha kable ha salog, echo o lanog, ug trapik ha agi-an. An katuyu-an san on-site spatial ug event lifecycle observation kay an pagdokumento san matuod nga proseso san pag-setup, agi-an san tawo, ug paghipos san okasyon para makahimo san sakto nga sukdanan ha interior modular layouts.*

### 3. Type of Research Intervention / Klase san Partisipasyon
This research intervention consists of **direct, non-participatory field observation and spatial mapping**. The researcher acts strictly as a passive, non-intrusive observer and will **NOT** interfere with your event program, client interactions, decorator setups, vendor coordination, or crew operations. The observation shadows the event lifecycle across three distinct operational phases:
* **Ingress (Load-In & Setup Phase):** Documenting delivery access, furniture hauling routes, ladder/truss rigging methods, floor cabling, and setup turnaround duration.
* **In-Session (Active Event Execution Phase):** Observing lobby registration flow, aisle circulation, service passage clearances (e.g., catering tray flow), visual sightlines, and perceived acoustic clarity.
* **Egress (Teardown & Turnover Phase):** Documenting crowd exit flow, furniture breakdown/stacking, loading bay vehicle access, and hall turnover speed.  

*Waraynon: Ini nga partisipasyon kay usa nga **direkta ug non-participatory field observation ug spatial mapping**. An mag-aaram kay magpapadayon la nga passive ug mahilom nga observer, ug **DIRI** mangugulang ha iyo programa, kliyente, dekorasyon, o trabaho san mga crew. An pag-obserbar kay magtututok ha tulo ka bahin san okasyon: Ingress (pagkarga ug pag-setup), In-Session (an mismong pagdumala san okasyon ug agi-an san tawo), ug Egress (an paghipos, pag-disassemble san muebles, ug paglimpyo san hall).*

### 6. Study Procedures / Pamaagi san Pag-aram
If you agree to grant observational access, the researcher will observe the following procedural guidelines:
1. **Pre-Event Coordination:** The researcher will coordinate with you regarding arrival times, scheduled load-in, program start, and teardown schedules.
2. **Passive Presence:** The researcher will wear proper identification and remain in non-obtrusive perimeter locations, adhering strictly to venue safety standards.
3. **Discrete Data Recording:** The researcher will record operational timestamps (e.g., arrival, unload, assembly, readiness) and field notes in a written observation log.
4. **Architectural Clearances:** Non-disruptive laser distance measurements (measuring aisle widths, door clear openings, and stage sightlines) will be taken without obstructing pedestrian or crew circulation.
5. **Non-Identifying Photography:** If permitted, wide-angle documentary photographs will capture spatial layout typologies, cable runs, furniture arrangements, and material conditions. In strict compliance with privacy standards, **all attendee and crew faces will be digitally blurred or excluded**, and proprietary commercial branding will be de-identified.  

*Waraynon: Kon matugot kamo, an mag-aaram kay magasunod san mga masunod nga pamaagi: (1) Makikig-koordinar ha iyo mahiunong ha oras san load-in, programa, ug teardown; (2) Magsusul-ot san ID ug matindog ha ligid agud diri makaulang; (3) Magsusulat san oras ug notes ha observation log; (4) Maghuhimo san diri makakaulang nga pagsukol san agi-an gamit an laser distance meter; (5) Kon tutugutan, maglilikrato san layout ug kable pero **i-b-blur an ngatanan nga nawong san tawo** agud maprotektaran an privacy.*

### 12. Confidentiality & Data Protection (RA 10173 Compliance) / Pagpreserbar san Datos ug Konpidensyalidad
In strict adherence to the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** and the ethics mandates of the University of San Carlos Research Ethics Committee (USC-REC):
* **Event De-Identification:** Observation records, timestamp logs, and spatial sketches will be labeled using alphanumeric event codes (e.g., Event Obs-01, Event Obs-02) rather than private client names or host organizations.
* **Photographic Privacy:** Any photographs taken for technical documentation (documenting furniture arrangements, cable runs, and material finishes) will have all human faces digitally blurred or obscured prior to inclusion in any manuscript or presentation. No identifying imagery of private individuals or unreleased commercial decor designs will be publicized.
* **Secure Storage:** Physical observation logs will be stored in a locked cabinet at the researcher's primary residence. Digital files and photographs will be encrypted using AES-256 bit algorithms and stored on password-protected hard drives with two-factor authentication.
* **Retention & Disposal:** All observation notes, raw measurement logs, and unblurred photo files will be retained for one (1) year following thesis completion, after which hard copies will be cross-cut shredded and digital files permanently deleted.  

*Waraynon: Sunod ha **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173)** ug ha mga pagsulondan san USC-REC: (1) An mga talaan kay gagamitan hin code (sugad san Event Obs-01) imbes nga tinuod nga ngaran san kliyente; (2) An tanan nga litrato kay **i-b-blur an mga nawong san tawo**; (3) An mga papel kay itatago ha nakakandado nga cabinet ug an digital files kay protektado san password; (4) Paglabay san usa (1) ka tuig pagkahuman san thesis, an tanan nga papel kay i-g-gupit (shred) ug an digital files kay permanente nga i-d-delete.*
