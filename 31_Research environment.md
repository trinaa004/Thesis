The Calbayog City Convention Center, also locally known as the 'convention center', is the only convention center in Calbayog City. It is located in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar and was originally a bus station before being renovated into a convention space on December 14, 2009. Following its renovation, the facility functions as a versatile, multi-use environment capable of accommodating diverse event formats for up to 500 occupants. Over the years, it has served the local community by hosting a diverse range of activities such as seminars, trade shows, weddings, birthdays, graduations, oath-taking ceremonies, national conventions, job fairs, and various training programs,  actively positioning itself as a regional hub for MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) events, cultural tourism, and civic assemblies.
The physical application of this study will focus strictly on the 840 sqm interior envelope of the venue, comprising the 711 sqm Main Convention Hall and the 129 sqm Lobby / pre-function area. The Main Hall currently operates as an open multi-purpose volume with ceramic tile flooring, perimeter glass walls lined with old existing drapery, and suspended fabric ceiling baffles that suffer from material age and lack of maintenance. The adjoining Lobby serves as the primary circulation threshold, registration zone, and guest receiving area. Both spaces currently lack integrated modular zoning, adaptable Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment (FF&E), an adjustable overhead ceiling track interface, and localized cultural interior identity. Focusing on this specific locale allows for the creation of an empirically grounded and site-specific interior design framework.
