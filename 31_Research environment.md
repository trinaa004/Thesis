The **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center** (officially chartered by the City Government of Calbayog, and locally referenced as the Calbayog City Convention Center or "the convention center") is the premier city-owned indoor convention facility in Calbayog City. Located in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar, the facility was originally constructed as a municipal central bus station before being officially renovated and inaugurated into a civic convention space on December 14, 2009. According to the official portal of the City Government of Calbayog (n.d.), the facility is formally envisioned to serve as a vital hub for promoting Calbayog City’s rich culture, arts, and tourism—featuring spaces for performances, art displays, cultural training, community gatherings, and local product showcases, while actively preserving regional traditions.

To contextualize the facility's specific operational role within Calbayog City's broader public event infrastructure, the municipal venue network is delineated across three distinct facilities:
1. **Calbayog City Sports Center**: A high-capacity sports and assembly arena designed for mass spectatorship, which officially accommodates large-scale athletic meets, city-wide assemblies, and the major outdoor street dancing competitions and grand showdowns of the **Hadang Festival (historically and popularly recognized as the Sarakiki-Hadang Festival)**.
2. **Handumanan Museum / Arts Gallery**: The city's dedicated municipal facility for preserving permanent historical artifacts, archival exhibits, and curated visual arts collections.
3. **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center**: Operates as an intimate, fully air-conditioned, and cost-effective 840-square-meter civic convention venue capable of accommodating up to 500 occupants. Because it provides a lower-overhead, fully enclosed rental tier compared to the sports arena, it functions as the city's primary venue for government assemblies, legislative caravans (such as the *PAGPASAMWAK Sangguniang Panlungsod Caravan*), regional economic summits (such as the *Samar Investment Forum*), academic conferences, private social banquets, and curated indoor cultural presentations and convocations (such as the landmark Samar-wide *Artists Kolektib* assembly).

The physical application of this study focuses strictly on the **840-square-meter interior envelope** of the venue, comprising the 711-square-meter Main Convention Hall and the 129-square-meter Lobby / pre-function area. The Main Hall currently operates as an open, unzoned multi-purpose volume featuring hard ceramic tile flooring, reflective perimeter glass walls lined with aged drapery, and an unbaffled ceiling volume lacking acoustic absorption, resulting in severe acoustic reverberation. The adjoining Lobby serves as the primary circulation threshold, registration zone, and guest reception area. Both spaces currently lack integrated modular zoning, adaptable Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment (FF&E), an adjustable overhead ceiling track interface, and localized cultural interior identity. Focusing on this specific 840 sqm interior envelope allows for the formulation of an empirically grounded, non-structural interior design framework and layout manual that optimizes operational efficiency and civic identity.
