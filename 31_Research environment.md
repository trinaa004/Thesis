The Calbayog City Convention Center, also locally known as the 'convention center', is the only convention center in Calbayog City. It is located in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar and was originally a bus station before being renovated into a convention space on December 14, 2009. Following its renovation, the facility functions as a versatile, multi-use environment capable of accommodating diverse event formats for up to 500 occupants. Over the years, it has served the local community by hosting a diverse range of activities such as seminars, trade shows, weddings, birthdays, graduations, oath-taking ceremonies, national conventions, job fairs, and various training programs,  actively positioning itself as a regional hub for MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions) events, cultural tourism, and civic assemblies.
The physical application of this study will focus strictly on the interior envelope of the venue’s Main Convention Hall, encompassing a total floor area of 840 square meters. The Main Hall currently operates as an open multi-purpose volume but lacks integrated modular zoning, adaptable Furniture, Fixtures, and Equipment (FF&E), overhead track infrastructure, and localized cultural interior identity. Focusing on this specific locale allows for the creation of an empirically grounded and site-specific interior design framework.
