### Section 4: Qualitative Feedback & Actionable Refinements

**1. What is the single most critical spatial bottleneck, clearance pinch-point, or operational flaw you observed in the proposed layouts?** — 9 responses

- **P1:** The setup time targets are optimistic for weddings and debuts. The venue furniture and the rigging can be fast, I agree, but styling (florals, ceiling swags, backdrop) still needs a prep day. Also, where do the modular stage and nesting tables go when a supplier brings their own furniture?
- **P2:** Storage. An storage room san Convention yana diri kasya an modular stage risers, nesting tables, ngan mobile screens nga sabay-sabay. Kailangan dedicated storage ngan inventory system.
- **P3:** An load san overhead grid. Kailangan structural verification san City Engineering before mag-install, ngan nakasurat an load rating san kada hoist point. Plus an power drops — dedicated circuits, kay pirme nagti-trip an breaker.
- **P4:** The stage side and the ramp. Also in 2A, some participants in each cluster will face away from the screen during lecture parts.
- **P5:** Wara ako nakit-an nga major bottleneck sa layout. Sa cultural side la: an iconography san Hadang ngan san Sarakiki kailangan klaro, diri magkasarumpay.
- **P6:** Durability of natural tikog in a hall that is humid and not always air-conditioned (mold, bukbok). And the MCM 'banig' cladding is only an imitation — it does not give income to our weavers.
- **P7 brgy:** An aircon sa main hall. Diri man gin-improve, an lobby la. Mapaso gihapon ha luyo pag puno.
- **P8:** For live music, the room might become too 'dead' once all the panels and baffles are in. And the stage might still be small for a full orchestra.
- **P9:** The 1:12 ramp for a 900 mm stage is about 10.8 m long (without landings yet). Also no companion seat beside the wheelchair bays, and the route to the CR is still not clear to me.

**2. If you were running a live event or managing the facility, what specific interior dimension, furniture specification, or finish would you modify?** — 9 responses

- **P1:** Keep all permanent finishes neutral (beige, warm white, wood) so we can style over them. Add visibility markers on any remaining glass and put proper CR signages.
- **P2:** Floor studs flush and rounded; casters with brakes; color-code the stage risers per height (300/600/900) para diri magkasarumpay an custodial.
- **P3:** Recessed cord reels with locking, para diri magbitay ha atubangan san LED wall o san camera. Then usa ka power distribution point ha backstage para sa mga suppliers.
- **P4:** Half-moon seating per cluster for seminars. Default stage height 600 mm. No fixed stairs on the stage sides so the scaffolding can sit flush against the stage wall.
- **P5:** Small didactic plaques beside the banig panels and the mural explaining the lara/samat weaves and the festival. Reusable printed fabric banners on the soffit rail for event titles instead of tarpaulins.
- **P6:** Use real tikog / banig on the mobile acoustic screens, commissioned from Samar weaving cooperatives, treated with fire retardant and anti-fungal coating, and made as replaceable panels.
- **P7 brgy:** Wara na ako iba nga ma-suggest. Okay na ak san gin-present.
- **P8:** Make the drapery adjustable so it can be opened for music events. Add monitor and power points at the stage edge.
- **P9:** Switchback ramp with landings and handrails on both sides, non-slip surface. Companion seat beside each wheelchair bay. Pictogram signage to the CR (with braille if possible).

**3. Additional recommendations for the City Government of Calbayog regarding facility operations:** — 9 responses

- **P1:** Allow the AC to be turned on during setup, even partially, or provide industrial fans. Include the layout templates in the booking packet so clients understand the real capacity.
- **P2:** Coordination san GSO, Engineering, ngan Planning para sa implementation ngan maintenance budget. Training san custodial staff para sa modular furniture ngan hoists.
- **P3:** Mag-hire o mag-train hin venue technician para sa hoists. Himuon an standard rigging rules para sa ngatanan nga suppliers.
- **P4:** Publish a simple booking manual with the templates and capacities. It will make client approvals faster.
- **P5:** Involve CACO and local artists in the final content of the murals and the waterfall baffles.
- **P6:** Procure the woven panels through DTI and the Samar weaving co-ops. Rotate artisan displays in the lobby concourse.
- **P7 brgy:** Ayuson an aircon sa main hall.
- **P8:** Let local performers and youth orchestras use the stage, and do an acoustic tuning test after installation.
- **P9:** Conduct an accessibility audit with PWD groups after the renovation, before it opens again.
