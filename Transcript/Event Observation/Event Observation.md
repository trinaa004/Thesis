# Protocol B: Multi-Phase Event Lifecycle Observation Matrix (Live Event Shadowing)
*Empirical Field Observation Transcripts & On-Site Shadowing Logs (Calbayog Cultural Convention Center)*

> **Methodological Context:** Administered during scheduled live events across contrasting typologies (Plenary Assembly, Social Banquet, and Trade Exhibition).  
> **Chronological Scope:** All 5 live shadowed events documented below occurred across a compressed 13-day period in October 2026 (October 5 to October 17, 2026), capturing real-world operational turnaround, calendar compression, and spatial reconfigurations firsthand.

---

## Observed Event 1: PAGPASAMWAK Sangguniang Panlungsod Legislative Caravan

### Event Overview & Booking Parameters
* **Name of Event:** PAGPASAMWAK Sangguniang Panlungsod Legislative Caravan
* **Client / Proponent:** LGU (Sangguniang Panlungsod / City Government of Calbayog)
* **Estimated Occupancy:** 300 pax
* **Event Typology:** Banquet type / Mixed-use civic caravan
* **Existing Furniture Used:** Plastic monobloc chairs with a fabric seat cover, foldable 6-seater round tables with tablecloth covers
* **Existing Lighting Condition:** Used outsourced lights and equipment to rig/mount the lights
* **Date & Time Observed:**
  * **October 5, 2026:**
    * Ingress: 6:00 pm – 8:00 pm
  * **October 6, 2026:**
    * During the Event: 8:00 am – 1:00 pm
    * Egress: 4:30 pm – 6:00 pm for booths and cleanup only (Venue clearing started ~1:30 pm)

---

### 1. Ingress Phase (Load-In & Setup Logistics)
* Started setting up the night before (October 5, 6:00 pm – 8:00 pm).
* They didn't cover up the ceiling, so it was a faster setup for the event.
* They put a lot more effort into the walkthrough gallery in the lobby.

---

### 2. In-Session Phase (Active Event Operations)

#### Main Hall:
* Registration for the event started at 8:00 am and the program started at around 9:00 am.
* The hosts and the people involved with the event didn’t really go up the stage during the initial event; it was only used for specific parts:
  * Used the stage and the podium at the second half of the program (9:00 am – 11:00 am).
  * First half of the program used the floor area by the stage; they placed a low platform to highlight the important guests / presenters’ table with seats (11:00 am – 12:00 pm).
  * During the second half of the program, they moved down the podium to the low platform on the floor area by the stage for a special part of the program.
  * After that part, a production number was performed on the floor area by the stage; the stage wasn’t used for this part because it wasn’t wide enough due to the LED screens and decor already occupying the stage. During this time, the outsourced lights were dimmed for their entry.
* The existing lights were off for some parts and were only turned on while waiting for the program to start.
* They used outsourced lights and equipment to control the general ambiance of the main hall of the convention center:
  * The outsourced lights used on the stage were hanging stage lights mounted on the outsourced aluminum lighting trusses.
  * Lights used on the stage: hanging spotlights with a moving head beam and hanging linear flood lights.
  * Spotlights with moving head beams were also placed on the floor of the stage.
  * Spotlights with moving head beams were also placed on top of the AC units along Column Grids C and D.
* The speakers were placed on both sides of the stage.
* The lights and sounds crew / tech booth was located on the left side, near the electrical room.
* A lot of people were lined up at the buffet table on both sides; the space is cramped but people find their own ways to get around.
* The waiters had to dodge and weave a lot to go around the space.

#### Foyer / Lobby:
* The registration table was located here and covered the second main entry/exit of the convention center, so only one door was accessible here.
* There were concessionaires (coffee and food stalls), a photo-taking area, and on the other side was the gallery walkthrough.

---

### 3. Egress Phase (Teardown & Turnover Logistics)
* During egress, the exit flow in the lobby area was crowded since the other entry/exit door was blocked by the registration table, and both sides of the lobby were also occupied by people looking at the exhibit.
* The event ended around 12:30 pm – 1:00 pm.
* They started clearing out the venue around 1:30 pm and finished at around 4:00 pm (booths and final cleanup extended to 4:30 pm – 6:00 pm).

## Observed Event 2: Samar Investment Forum & Investor's Gala

### Part A: Samar Investment Forum (Daytime Forum & Trade Exhibition)

#### Event Overview & Booking Parameters
* **Name of Event:** Samar Investment Forum
* **Client / Proponent:** LGU (Province of Samar & City Government of Calbayog)
* **Estimated Occupancy:** 150–200 pax
* **Event Typology:** Multi-Use Hybrid
  * *Lobby / Pre-Function:* Trade Fair (exhibition) showcasing all the features and products in the provinces of Samar (Calbayog, Catbalogan, Daram, Basey, and the Province of Samar).
  * *Main Hall:* Banquet-type setup with utilization of the stage.
* **Existing Furniture Used:** Cushioned steel seats in white, foldable 6-seater round tables with tablecloth covers.
* **Existing Lighting Condition:** Used outsourced lights and equipment to rig/mount the lights.
* **Date & Time Observed:**
  * **October 10–11, 2026 (Ingress):** Setup started Oct 10 in the evening, continued Oct 11 noon til early morning 2:00–3:00 am; additional ingress Oct 11, 5:00 pm – 8:00 pm.
  * **October 12, 2026 (During Event):** 8:00 am – 3:00 pm.
  * **October 12, 2026 (Egress / Reset):** 4:30 pm – 6:00 pm for booths and cleanup (reconfigured for evening Gala).

#### 1. Ingress Phase (Load-In & Setup Logistics)
* They started setting up on October 10 in the evening and continued October 11 noontime til early morning around 2:00–3:00 am.
* The curtains didn’t have a curtain tieback, so they had to look for other ways to open up the curtains so that the doorways would be open during ingress; for some areas they were able to move the curtains to the side successfully, but for some they just tied it manually.
* For the stage lights, they outsourced their own aluminum lighting trusses with an Aluminum Lighting Crank Stand Truss.
* The ceiling was mostly left alone and wasn’t completely covered; only a few ceiling drapes / swagging were present, and the light fixtures were used as anchors for it. The "DIY" anchors from previous use that were on the walls were also used as anchors. They did not have to make their own framing for a fabric covering for the ceiling like other events, so it didn't take too much time.
* They used the side sliding doors for putting the things they needed for the event inside; they mostly used the right side.

#### 2. In-Session Phase (Active Event Operations)
* When people or staff want to utilize the sliding door side exits in the main hall area, they had to keep looking for where the ends between the curtains were so that they could get out of the venue, because the curtain rods didn’t make it easy to open the curtains to enter with ease.
* Lighting would change between different parts of the program, but the original overhead lights of the convention center are usually turned off and only the outsourced lights are used.
* The ambient lights would be blue throughout the program, and would turn to a warm white color during breaks and during lunch so guests were able to see clearly.
* The hanging stage lights were mounted on the outsourced aluminum lighting trusses; the lights used on stage were hanging spotlights with a moving head beam, hanging linear flood lights, and hanging PAR lights. Additional hanging linear flood lights were placed facing the audience / guests.
* Strobe lights were mounted on the floor by the stage facing the audience/guests for additional ambiance; when turned on with a warm white color temperature, these were used to light the audience/guest area during breaks and lunch.
* Two spotlights with a moving head beam were placed on top of the AC units by the columns on Column Grid C, facing the audience/guests, pointed to the ceiling to add ambient light.
* The flow of traffic is one-way since they blocked one of the two main doors in the lobby area with a booth (due to exhibition booths in the lobby area).
* There is no dedicated fire exit signage.
* There was a bit of a bottleneck during the start of the event as all the guests were coming in.
* The "Palawan Gold" table was placed by the one main entrance that wasn't blocked, and a lot of people wanted to check out the jewelry they had there, with some blocking the door.
* The registration table is in the main hall near the lobby; there is enough space for the line queue buildup.
* In the beginning of the program, the 2-meter middle walkway allocated between the banquet tables was blocked by cameras on tripods for the livestream.
* Traffic is manageable but the paths are just enough for one way.
* Space between seats to seats is a bit too tight, around 700–750 mm.
* The sightlines at the back are a bit blocked by the Aluminum Lighting Crank Stand Truss, but there are 2 LED screens on the sides that show the speaker being blocked.
* The emcees can be heard; audio isn't muffled, with little to no echo.
* A bit dim inside because existing main lights are off and they relied on the outsourced lights for the general ambiance.
* It gets warm near the middle of the venue and cold if you are near and in line with the AC units (AC temp is already set at 17 degrees Celsius).

#### 3. Egress Phase (Teardown & Turnover Logistics)
* Exit was crowded; some people weren't aware of side exits, so all traffic converged towards the singular double-door exit in the center because the other door was blocked.
* After the event, the booths started packing up their things, but not everything was cleaned up on the same day; it continued into the next day (October 13) before the start of the graduation event.
* Egress and cleaning up were held back until attendees were out of the venue: the event ended at 3:00 pm, and the egress of the booths started at 4:00 pm, as well as cleanup, because the same setup was going to be used for the Investor's Gala afterwards (scheduled start time of 6:30 pm). They only had a few hours to clean up and reset the space.

## Observed Event 3: TESDA Graduation Ceremony

### Event Overview & Booking Parameters
* **Name of Event:** TESDA Graduation
* **Client / Proponent:** DOLE / TESDA
* **Estimated Occupancy:** 80–100 pax
* **Event Typology:** Graduation ceremony / Plenary theater seating arrangement
* **Existing Furniture Used:** Plastic monobloc chairs with a fabric cover; 26-seat VIP banquet table in rear right.
* **Existing Lighting Condition:** Used the existing lights of the convention center.
* **Date & Time Observed:**
  * **October 13, 2026:**
    * Ingress: 8:00 am – 9:00/10:00 am (chairs & AV) and 1:00 pm – 2:00 pm (decor)
    * During the Event: 4:00 pm – 8:30 pm (Program delayed; actual start 5:30 pm)
    * Egress: 8:30 pm – 12:00 midnight

---

### 1. Ingress Phase (Load-In & Setup Logistics)
* Context: There were still remnants from the previous event the day before; by 8:00 am they started adding seats, and at 1:00 pm brought in decor.
* The decor and the event were both on the same day since the decor was simple.
* Used the center door; not that much decor, only a few hours til start because they started at 1:00 pm. Event was supposed to start at 4:00 pm but got delayed to 5:30 pm.
* No ceiling drapery; only stage decor with fake flowers and a few lights.
* Decoration in total only took a few hours since it was simple; chair placement started in the morning (8:00 am – 10:00 am) along with setup of the LED wall and audio.

---

### 2. In-Session Phase (Active Event Operations)
* 3:00 pm – 3:30 pm (supposed start): Not much queue buildup.
* Lots of empty space since the seats only reached the first half of the space; the second half is empty.
* Lobby isn't used at all.
* No waiters or catering during the main ceremony.
* Sightline is clear, nothing blocking it; stage height is good.
* There is echo—pretty bad, comparable to a church echo—but speech can still be understood.
* Comfortably cold inside the hall; only the lobby is warm.
* Entry flow: middle entrance straight to the registration table at the center-right side of the main hall, and then straight to the seats by Point A and Point B at the front, by the stage (the stage is directly in front of those two front points).
* Rear left side of the main hall is empty.
* Rear right of the main hall has the table with plates, preparing for a dinner after the graduation for guests (only seats 26 people).
* Some people used the center-right exit of the main hall to leave the venue for a bit during waiting.
* Graduates went into the lobby area before the graduation started and had to wait until 5:40 pm before getting called back to do the graduation processional walk to their seats from the lobby.
* During the handing of certificates, graduates go up from the left side and exit on the right side of the stage.

---

### 3. Egress Phase (Teardown & Turnover Logistics)
* During exit, it didn't create a bottleneck since the area is spacious enough and there weren't that many people.
* 26 seats were transferred to the dining area at the rear right of the main hall for a post-graduation dinner for the guests (not for the graduates).
* Not much congestion in general since this is a small event.
* Event ended around 8:30 pm.
* Cleanup started as the dinner for the guests was happening around 9:00 pm, and the hall was completely cleared out by 12:00 midnight.

## Observed Event 5: Wedding Event (Ceremony & Nuptial Banquet)

### Event Overview & Booking Parameters
* **Name of Event:** Wedding Event
* **Client / Proponent:** Private Client (not to be disclosed)
* **Estimated Occupancy:** 150 pax
* **Event Typology:** Social Banquet & Ceremony Hybrid
  * *Note:* The wedding ceremony and the wedding reception were both held in the convention center. After the wedding ceremony, the seats (plenary type) for the ceremony were re-positioned to join the banquet-style setup.
* **Existing Furniture Used:** Plastic monobloc chairs with a fabric seat cover, cushioned steel seats in white, foldable 6-seater round tables with tablecloth covers, and a dedicated loveseat for the couple.
* **Existing Lighting Condition:** Used outsourced lights and equipment to rig/mount the lights.
* **Date & Time Observed:**
  * **October 15–16, 2026 (Ingress):**
    * Ingress for ceiling focal point started October 15 around 8:00 pm, finished midnight.
    * General ingress October 16 from 1:00 pm – 5:00 pm (finished ~10:00 pm).
  * **October 17, 2026 (During Event):** 11:00 am – 8:00 pm (Ceremony started 3:00 pm, concluded 4:30 pm; reception followed until 8:00 pm).
  * **October 17–18, 2026 (Egress):** 8:00 pm – 11:00 pm (Ceiling fabric/truss teardown completed morning after, Oct 18, 8:00 am – 11:30 am).

---

### 1. Ingress Phase (Load-In & Setup Logistics)
* Ingress for the focal point of the event (the ceiling decor supported by outsourced aluminum steel trusses to place the fabric and design it for the wedding) started October 15 in the evening around 8:00 pm and finished around midnight.
* The ingress and setup for other major items resumed on October 16 at 1:00 pm (a day before the wedding).
* Catering also brought in their things around 1:00 pm; during this time, it was very humid inside because they are not allowed to turn the AC on during the ingress phase, so they prefer setting up in the evening because it’s not as hot.
* They started building the entryway for the wedding: because the entryways of the existing convention center from the foyer to the main hall are wide, they framed it smaller and added decorative overlays.
* For this, they only used the closest side entry on the left to bring in their things in preparation for the wedding.
* They brought their own scaffoldings to reach the ceiling when covering it up, and made their own framing out of aluminum wires to support it; they also utilized the "DIY" anchors that past event planners and crews had left on walls.
* The main floor was kept clear of wires.
* The door opening is too small and cannot be opened wider, so some items kept bumping into the door frame, causing potential damage; it was also noted that not all perimeter sliding doors are functional.
* Because they only used one entry point, all items were piled into that single area, but the space was large enough for workers on two different scaffoldings to work on the ceiling simultaneously. They finished covering the ceiling around 5:00–6:00 pm in the evening (having started around 2:00 pm).
* They also covered up the stage because it was not going to be utilized and they wanted the bride to be the sole main focus.
* They finished setting up everything for the next day at around 10:00 pm.

---

### 2. In-Session Phase (Active Event Operations)

#### Lobby / Pre-Function Area:
* Used as a holding area for guests before the wedding started: no seating provided, no AC running; guests were sweating and using their hands as fans.
* Left side of the lobby accommodated snacks and drinks; right side accommodated the photo booth area.
* The wedding ceremony was supposed to start at 2:00 pm, but the venue was not yet filled.

#### Main Hall:
* The focal point of the event (the ceiling decor supported by aluminum trusses) was placed along the center of the main hall, with a circular platform underneath for the ceremony.
* The wedding officially started at 3:00 pm.
* No issues with circulation since invitee numbers were moderate.
* The wedding ceremony concluded at 4:30 pm; the plenary/auditorium-type seats were removed from the area around the ceremony platform and placed back with the round tables for banquet-style seating.
* Plenty of space to move around.
* Temperature was cold directly adjacent to the AC units, but warm in areas away from them (uneven temperature distribution).
* Attendee and waiter circulation was good with wide aisles.
* Stage sightlines were clear, but the vertical aluminum trusses supporting the ceiling feature blocked the view of the couple from rear seats.
* Speech was audible; the fabric covering the ceiling buffered out room echoes.

---

### 3. Egress Phase (Teardown & Turnover Logistics)
* Egress commenced at 8:00 pm.
* By 8:30 pm, crews had removed the tables and chairs; general guests had departed, while the bride, groom, and close family remained in the venue.
* Catering began cleaning up.
* Video and photo/media teams began packing gear along the perimeter.
* Crews began stacking chairs and folding tables; by 8:40 pm, table clearing was nearly finished.
* Camera crew departed; catering continued cleanup.
* By 8:50 pm, all seats and tables were cleared out and positioned outside for truck load-in, utilizing the right-side entry/exit.
* Floor sweeping commenced; chairs in the center of the hall were moved to the sides to clear space for cleaning, with 3 staff members sweeping simultaneously to accelerate the process.
* At 9:00 pm, catering was packing up, cleared their stations, and prepared to exit via the main entry/exit.
* At 9:20 pm, crews began taking down lighting at the front dais where the couple had been seated. By this time, catering had completely packed up and exited.
* At this stage, only the lights and sounds crew (tech booth) remained to dismantle setups around the venue; they utilized floodlights on mobile stands to illuminate the hall since the ceiling lights were covered.
* The lights and sounds crew finished packing everything around 11:00 pm, as they did not need to wait for the event decorator crew to dismantle the heavy ceiling drapery and truss structures.
* The ceiling decor and outsourced aluminum truss framework were left in place overnight and dismantled the following morning (October 18), starting at 8:00 am and concluding around 11:30 am.
