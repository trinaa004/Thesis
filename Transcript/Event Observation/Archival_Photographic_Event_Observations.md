## 1. Methodological Framework & Observation Dimensions

When evaluating historical facility utilization across a 12-year trajectory (2014–2026), physical presence during past events is impossible. Under environmental design research epistemology (Zeisel, 2006; Groat & Wang, 2013), **Physical Trace Observation** allows the interior designer to systematically decode human spatial behavior, equipment adaptations, circulation barriers, and interior maintenance wear through high-resolution event photography and official textual captions.

For each selected exemplar event, observations are recorded across **five standardized spatial trace dimensions**:
1. **Seating Array & Furniture Layout Traces:** Seating density, parallel linear vs. angled geometries, row pitch, chair push-back clearances, table cluster arrangements, and perimeter dead spaces.
2. **Audio-Visual, Rigging & Utility Traces:** Footprints of ground-supported aluminum crank-stand trusses, loose tripod speaker stands, and trailing electrical cords taped across pedestrian walking paths.
3. **Acoustic & Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ) Traces:** Unpadded painted upper walls and ceiling, glazed porcelain floor tile reflections, bare envelope flutter echoes, and unconditioned lobby area.
4. **Cultural & Decorative Placemaking Traces:** Reliance on temporary, single-use printed vinyl tarpaulins and tape mountings versus permanent, culturally inspired interior architectural finishes (e.g., Wallcrown dried-tikog-inspired wallpaper, white MCM banig-weave textures, Hadang CNC wood relief carving).
5. **Circulation, Egress & Accessibility Traces:** Main entrance door clearance, lobby holding area crowding, waiter food-service lanes, fire exit portal clearances, and compliance with Batas Pambansa Blg. 344 (BP 344) wheelchair seating integration.

* **Master Ledger Index:** Event #3 in Category 4 / Ledger
* **Organizing Proponents:** Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) Samar Provincial Office, Philippine Trade Training Center (PTTC), and the City Government of Calbayog
* **Programmed Spatial Setup:** Classroom / Creative Workshop Training
* **Estimated Occupant Scale:** Tier 1/2 (53 Creative Entrepreneurs + Trainers and Facilitators; ~70–90 total occupants)
* **Archival Verification Sources:**
  - Official Philippine Information Agency (PIA) News Bulletin: [PIA News Article](https://pia.gov.ph/news/dti-samar-transforms-creative-talents-into-sustainable-businesses/)
  - City Government of Calbayog Official Press Release & Event Photo Log: [Calbayog PIO Link](https://www.facebook.com/100090744381505/posts/598712199830254)

### Textual & Caption Context
> *"CALBAYOG CITY, Samar (PIA) -- Fifty-three creative entrepreneurs from Samar gathered at the Calbayog City Convention Center for a specialized training aimed at transforming raw artistic talents into commercially viable enterprises. Spearheaded by DTI Samar in partnership with PTTC and the City Government, the workshop equipped local artisans, visual artists, and digital creators with foundational business, intellectual property, and product development competencies."*

---

### Structured Observational Audit (Researcher Personal Notes)

#### Dimension 1: Seating & Furniture Arrangement Traces
* **Observed Spatial Configuration:** Participants are seated at continuous linear folding tables arranged in parallel forward-facing rows oriented toward the stage and led wall.
* **Aisle & Pitch Clearances:** Center aisle measures approximately 1000 mm. Spacing between table rows provides limited push-back clearance (under 750 mm), making it difficult for seated participants to exit without disrupting neighboring attendees.
* **Spatial usage:** Because the group consists of 53 participants within a 711 sqm hall designed for 500, the front 30% of the room is densely occupied while the rear 70% was used as a gallery area showcasing differnt crafts like wood lase engravings and paintings. The lobby is left unused

#### Dimension 2: Audio-Visual, Rigging & Utility Traces
* **Floor Cabling:** no visible cords and power strips across the central and side floor aisles.
* **Projection & Audio Setup:** The audio and visual tech booth is still located on the side near the electrical room, the LED wall is used for the presentations

#### Dimension 3: Acoustic & Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ) Traces
* **Acoustic Reflections:** With bare painted masonry and unbaffled gypsum upper wall, vocal presentations might have suffered from high room reverberation (estimated RT₆₀ > 2.0 s), possibly causing speech to blur beyond row 4. Although the gallery display might help with buffering out the echo. 


#### Dimension 4: Cultural & Decorative Placemaking Traces
* **Stage & Wall Backdrops:** The stage platform features an LED wall used for the presentation across the rear wall. The interior exhibits no permanent Samar cultural textures, banig weave geometry, or Calbayog craft identity, presenting a generic, sterile aesthetic.

#### Dimension 5: Circulation, Egress & Accessibility Traces
* **Facilitator Circulation:** Unable to really tell with the photos but chair rows  and cross aisles are present. 
* **Accessibility (BP 344):** No dedicated wheelchair companion seating bays are present but there is enough clearance for them to move around freely since there aren't that many people in the venue.

* **Master Ledger Index:** Event #64 (and Event #63) in Category 1 & 2 / Ledger
* **Organizing Proponents:** City Government of Calbayog, City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO), and Calbayog ECCD Program
* **Programmed Spatial Setup:** Plenary / Theatrical Mass Assembly
* **Estimated Occupant Scale:** Tier 4 Peak Civic Capacity (~400–500 occupants: young moving-up pupils, teachers, parents, guardians, and city officials)
* **Archival Verification Sources:**
  - City Government of Calbayog Official PIO Photo Log: [Calbayog PIO Facebook Post](https://www.facebook.com/100084402231943/posts/647086221448113)
  - Companion Moving-Up Ceremony Session Archives: [Calbayog PIO Link](https://www.facebook.com/100084402231943/posts/646332164856852)

### Textual & Caption Context
> *"Child Development Centers of Barangay Cacaransan, Capoocan, Pagbalican and San Policarpo I and II during their Recognition Rites cum Moving-up Ceremony held at the Calbayog City Convention Center, this morning. City officials, day care workers, parents, and community stakeholders join in celebrating the foundational milestone of young Calbayognon learners."*

---

### Structured Observational Audit (Researcher Personal Notes)

#### Dimension 1: Seating & Furniture Arrangement Traces
* **Observed Spatial Configuration:** Wall-to-wall grid of loose, unlinked plastic Monobloc chairs arranged in straight, parallel rows nearly filling the entire 711 sqm Main Plenary Hall floor plate.
* **Sightline Obstructions:** Because the hall floor is entirely flat, parents and families seated beyond Row 6 endure severe visual sightline blockages (C-value estimated under 25 mm). Parents are observed standing up or leaning into aisles to photograph their children on stage.
* **Chair Shifts & Alignment:** Because individual chairs lack interlocking or ganging mechanisms, rows become misaligned as attendees adjust their seats, compressing row passage clearance down to under presumably 250 mm.

#### Dimension 2: Audio-Visual, Rigging & Utility Traces
* **stage Lighting:** the only stage lighting were the floor lamps by the backdrop decor on the stage 
* **Stage Cable Routing:** Cable routing is behind the backdrop decor of the stage, no hazzards to be seen.

#### Dimension 3: Acoustic & Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ) Traces
* **Crowd Noise & Vocal Reverberation:** With over 450 attendees and high acoustic energy from young children, ambient sound pressure levels rise substantially. Speech intelligibility through the PA system might be okay since during this year, the ceiling still had a few fabric ceilng baffles

#### Dimension 4: Cultural & Decorative Placemaking Traces
* **Stage Scenography:** The stage backdrop consists of a large custom vinyl tarpaulin stretched over a rented wooden frame, framed by rented balloon arches. No permanent Waray or Calbayog cultural design elements or architectural finishes are present.

#### Dimension 5: Circulation, Egress & Accessibility Traces
* **Crushing Aisle Bottleneck:** The entire 500-person assembly relies on a single central aisle measuring approximately 5500 mm in clear width. During the recessional march and post-ceremony dispersal, severe pedestrian gridlock might have occured at the transition between the Main Hall doors and the Lobby.
* **BP 344 Accessibility Failure:** Zero dedicated wheelchair parking bays are provided within the seating array. PWD grandparents and attendees with strollers might have been forced to park at the far rear perimeter near the loading doors, but none can be observed in the photos

### Structured Observational Audit (Researcher Personal Notes)

#### Dimension 1: Seating & Furniture Arrangement Traces
* **Observed Spatial Configuration:** High-density rectangular X-Y grid of linear banquet tables ( 10 persons per table) covering the main hall with a wide central aisle for the religious float or carroza to enter with ease

#### Dimension 2: Audio-Visual, Rigging & Utility Traces

* **Bare Ceiling:** The ceiling is kep bare and thay also just used the existing overhead lights but some lights weren't working 


#### Dimension 4: Cultural & Decorative Placemaking Traces
* **Ceremonial Décor:** Rich formal ceremonial styling, floral arrangements, and fraternity emblems; however, wall backdrops rely heavily on temporary decor rather than integrated interior features.

#### Dimension 5: Circulation, Egress & Accessibility Traces
* **Catering Service Collisions:** Catering wait-staff carrying 10-plate heavy trays must weave through the tight gaps between diner chairs, frequently brushing against guests or having to stop when chairs shift.

### Structured Observational Audit (Researcher Personal Notes)

#### Dimension 1: Seating & Furniture Arrangement Traces
* **Observed Spatial Configuration:** Linear perimeter arrangement where cooking demonstration tables stations are against perimeter walls with ample space from the wall, in the room's central zone are plenary theater type seating for guests after during the judging /evaluation phase, about 2.4 meters from the first row of seats is a long linear table for the dished to be placed before  judging /evaluation and just by the stage, the judges' table is placed, it's is only a two seater rectangular table placed parallel to the stage and the long linear table for the dished to be placed before  judging /evaluation. the stage is only used for the LED wall where the timer was displayed during the cooking part of the program.

* **Pedestrian Flow Friction:** Visitors gather around the demonstration stations in the aisles between the seating and the cooking demonstration tables, there is enough space to go around, but during the showcase of the dishes since it's only one linear table and the guests trying to look at the dishes all gather in that one area creating congestion.

#### Dimension 2: Audio-Visual, Rigging & Utility Traces
* **Utility Routing for Cooking & Display:** the used butane stoves, so no gas tanks 


#### Dimension 3: Acoustic & Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ) Traces
* **Ventilation & Odor Dissipation:** The indoor preparation of smoked fish dishes within an enclosed interior envelope might expose significant mechanical exhaust deficits. The facility lacks localized exhaust hoods, resulting in lingering food smoke and thermal buildup.


#### Dimension 4: Cultural & Decorative Placemaking Traces
* **Culinary Heritage Opportunity:** While the culinary content represents Calbayog's core gastronomic heritage (*tinapa* production), the physical interior shell provides zero interior support—no regional weave textures, no local timber millwork, and no contextual storytelling panels.

#### Dimension 5: Circulation, Egress & Accessibility Traces
* **Lobby Entry Obstruction:** registration booths are positioned inside the 129 sqm Lobby, the main antry isnt blocked
