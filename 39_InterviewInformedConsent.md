### 2. Purpose of the Research / Katuyu-an san Pag-aram
The purpose of this study is to formulate a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework for the 840 square meter Main Hall of the Calbayog City Convention Center in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. The qualitative interview aims to gather in-depth operational insights, setup turnaround experiences, acoustic challenges, ceiling track rigging requirements, and cultural heritage perspectives from key event industry professionals and venue administrators.  
*Waraynon: An katuyu-an san ini nga pag-aram kay an paghimo san usa nga non-structural interior design framework para ha 840 square meter nga Main Hall san Calbayog City Convention Center ha Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. An qualitative interview kay nagtutuyo ha pag-kolekta san ma-klares ug ha-lalom nga eksperyensya ha pamalakad, mga problema ha turnaround ug setup, lanog (echo), ceiling track rigging, ug disenyo ha kultura tikang ha mga eksperto ha event industry ug venue administrators.*

### 3. Type of Research Intervention / Klase san Partisipasyon
This research involves your participation in an individual, semi-structured qualitative interview session. With your explicit permission, the interview will be audio-recorded to ensure precise transcription and qualitative thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006 framework).  
*Waraynon: Ini nga pag-aram kay nagkikinahanglan san imo partisipasyon ha usa nga individual semi-structured qualitative interview. Kon ikaw magtutugot, an interview kay i-a-audio record para ha sakto nga pag-transcribe ug thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006 framework).*
