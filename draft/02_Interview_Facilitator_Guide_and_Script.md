## Guide Overview & Methodological Rationale

This guide provides a standardized, verbatim field script for conducting qualitative semi-structured key informant interviews for the undergraduate interior design thesis study on the **Calbayog City Convention Center**.

### Why Questioning Routes are Differentiated by Role:
In qualitative research methodology (Patton, 2015; Morse, 2015; Creswell & Poth, 2018), interviewing key informants across diverse professional backgrounds requires **purposive thematic targeting**:
* **Event Planners, Coordinators, & Venue Operators/Technicians (Route A)** possess specialized, first-hand expertise in physical setup/teardown turnaround times, furniture hauling, loading door pinch points, aisle clearances, overhead rigging hoists, and lighting/sound controls.
* **Cultural Advocates, Tourism Officers, Historians, & Local Artists (Route B)** possess specialized expertise in regional Samar heritage, Basey *banig* weaving traditions, *tikog* grass craft preservation, Calbayog waterfall iconography, civic pride, and aesthetic versatility.
* *Methodological Rule:* Asking cultural advocates technical questions regarding table stacking dollies or motorized ceiling hoists produces speculative, artificial data outside their professional scope. Route B deliberately focuses on heritage integration, civic atmosphere, and visual harmony, maximizing the value of their specialized knowledge.

## 5. Questioning Route A: Event Planners, Coordinators, & Venue Operators

*Administer this full 11-question route for participants who manage, coordinate, or operate events and logistics inside the hall.*

### Module 1: Existing Operational & Setup Bottlenecks (SPOP Q1.1 & Q1.2)

#### Question 1 (Spatial & Ingress Challenges)
* **English:** *"What are the biggest space and layout challenges you face when setting up and packing down events in the convention center?"*
* **Waray:** *"Ano an mga pinaka-dako nga problema ha lugar nga iyo naieksperyensyahan samtang nag-aandam o naghihipos san okasyon ha convention center?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Ingress/Loading:* Is the loading entrance too narrow or congested when unloading trucks?
  - *Lobby:* Does the 129 sqm entrance lobby get overcrowded during guest registration?
  - *Staging:* Is there enough temporary space to store chairs and boxes while decorating?

#### Question 2 (Turnaround Duration & Delays)
* **English:** *"On average, how many hours does it take your team to set up the hall, and what causes the longest delays?"*
* **Waray:** *"Pira ka oras an kasagaran nga gin-gagamit niyo ha pag-andam san hall, ug ano an mga buruhaton nga pinaka-nagpapadugay ha iyo?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Labor:* How many crew members are needed to arrange the 500 chairs or heavy banquet tables?
  - *Floor Cabling:* Does taping electrical cords and sound cables along the floor waste hours of setup time?
  - *Stage Setup:* Does building custom backdrops from scratch take up most of the turnover window?

#### Question 3 (IEQ: Sound, Lighting, & Air Comfort)
* **English:** *"How do the current sound clarity, lighting, and room temperature affect your events?"*
* **Waray:** *"Paunan-o nakaka-apekto an lanog san tunog, suga, ug temperatura san hangin ha padalagan san iyo mga okasyon?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Acoustics:* Is there severe room echo (reverberation) bouncing off the ceramic tiles and glass walls during speeches?
  - *Lighting:* Are existing fixtures too dim for reading, or do they create harsh glare on presentation screens?
  - *Ventilation:* Does the hall become stuffy or warm during peak 500-occupant attendance?

---

### Module 2: Spatial Modularity & Furniture Flexibility (SPOP Q2)

#### Question 4 (Movable FF&E & Modular Staging)
* **English:** *"What kinds of movable furniture or adjustable staging would make your event setups faster and easier?"*
* **Waray:** *"Ano nga klase san mga nagigius nga muebles o nababalyo nga entablado (stage) an makakapadali ug makakapa-abtik san iyo pag-ayos?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Tables/Chairs:* Would lightweight folding tables or stackable chairs with rolling dollies help?
  - *Partitions:* Would movable acoustic space dividers help you host smaller meetings in half the hall?
  - *Stage:* Would modular staging blocks that adjust in height improve rear sightlines?

#### Question 5 (Standard Layout Templates & Manual)
* **English:** *"How would having ready-made layout templates (for theater, banquet, classroom, or trade booths) help your event planning?"*
* **Waray:** *"Paunan-o makakabulig ha iyo pagplano kon may daan na nga giya o plano san mga ayos (sugad san theater, bangkete, o booths)?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Planning:* Would it speed up approvals with local clients and government organizers?
  - *Accuracy:* Would pre-calculated floor plans eliminate trial-and-error measuring during ingress?

#### Question 6 (Aisle Clearance, Catering Paths, & Wheelchair Accessibility)
* **English:** *"What problems do you notice with aisle spacing, waiter service paths, or wheelchair accessibility during large gatherings?"*
* **Waray:** *"Ano nga mga problema ha agi-an, pagserbi san mga waiters, o agi-an san wheelchair para ha PWD an agsub niyo maobserbaran ha mga dako nga okasyon?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Catering:* Do waiters carrying heavy food trays struggle to navigate between banquet tables?
  - *Accessibility:* Are wheelchair ramps and 1200 mm clear paths available for persons with disabilities (BP 344)?

---

### Module 3: Overhead Ceiling Track & Rigging Systems (SPOP Q3.1)

#### Question 7 (Ceiling-Mounted Utility Tracks & Rigging)
* **English:** *"How would ceiling-mounted tracks or hanging points help your lighting, sound, and backdrop setups?"*
* **Waray:** *"Paunan-o makakabulig ha iyo kon may daan na nga mga tracks o kabitayan ha ceiling para ha suga, sound, ug mga dekorasyon?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Hanging Decor:* Would it make hanging ceiling drapes, fabric swags, and banners safer and faster without tall ladders?
  - *Stage Lighting:* Would overhead lighting tracks improve spotlight angles for performances?

#### Question 8 (Motorized Overhead Hoists vs. Floor Tripods)
* **English:** *"How would motorized overhead hoists (which lift equipment to the ceiling) improve safety and free up floor space?"*
* **Waray:** *"Paunan-o makakabulig an motorized hoists (nga nag-aalsa san gamit pakadto ha ceiling) ha kaluwasan ug ha pagpahaluag san salog?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Safety:* Does eliminating speaker tripods and lighting stands prevent guests from tripping?
  - *Floor Space:* Does hoisting equipment overhead free up usable square meters for more guest seating?

---

### Module 4: Cultural and Natural Heritage Integration & Future-Proof Specifications (SPOP Q3.1 & Q3.2)

#### Question 9 (Integrating Samar Banig & Calbayog Waterfall Motifs)
* **English:** *"What are your thoughts on featuring Samar cultural heritage (Basey banig weaves) and Calbayog natural heritage (waterfall motifs) on the walls and ceiling?"*
* **Waray:** *"Ano an imo panlantaw ha pagbutang san disenyo tikang ha aton kultural nga kabilin (banig san Basey) ug natural nga kabilin (mga busay san Calbayog) ha mga dingding ug ceiling?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Acoustic Panels:* How do you feel about acoustic wall panels textured with woven *tikog* reed patterns?
  - *Ceiling Baffles:* What do you think of fluid, cascading ceiling sound baffles inspired by Calbayog waterfalls?

#### Question 10 (Balancing Local Heritage with Client Motifs)
* **English:** *"How can we design these local heritage elements so they look distinctive without clashing with a client's custom wedding or conference theme?"*
* **Waray:** *"Paunan-o naton maipapakita ini nga mga disenyo nga diri makikig-away o makikig-bunggo ha tema san kliyente (sugad san kasal o pormal nga seminar)?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Clashing:* Have you ever experienced permanent venue decorations clashing with a bride's color motif or a corporate banner?
  - *Neutral Finishes:* Would subtle geometric wood engravings and neutral earth tones work better than bright painted murals?

#### Question 11 (Durable, Easy-to-Clean Materials & Rapid Turnaround)
* **English:** *"In your experience, what kinds of durable and easy-to-clean materials does the venue need to withstand heavy event wear and tear?"*
* **Waray:** *"Sumala ha imo eksperyensya, ano nga mga lig-on ug masayon-limpyohan nga materyales an kinahanglan san hall para magpabilin nga maopay bisan agsub gamiton?"*
* **Interviewer Probes (Prompt if needed):**
  - *Wear Points:* Which parts of the hall get scuffed, kicked, or stained the fastest during setups?
  - *Maintenance:* What wall finishes or fabrics are easiest to wipe down and sanitize between consecutive event bookings?

## 6. Questioning Route B: Cultural Advocates, Tourism Officers, & Artists

*Administer this focused, specialized route for cultural stakeholders, local historians, tourism officers, and local artisans. It omits physical furniture logistics and focuses deeply on heritage authenticity, placemaking, civic pride, and visual versatility.*

### Transition Statement (Read to Participant):
> *"Because of your specialized expertise in local Samar heritage, arts, and tourism, our questions will focus specifically on how the convention center's interior can honor authentic regional identity, celebrate Calbayog's natural landmarks, and create a welcoming, proud civic atmosphere for our community and visitors."*  
>  
> *Waraynon: "Tungod san imo dako nga kaabtik ha kultura, arte, ug turismo san aton bungto, an aton mga pakiana kay magtututok ha kon paunan-o an interior san convention center makakapatahum ug makakapasidungog san aton kultural ug natural nga kabilin ha pamaagi nga nagpapadako san aton bugal."*

---

### Core Question 1 (General Ambiance & Civic Dignity — from Module 1 Q3)
* **English:** *"When you attend civic assemblies, cultural presentations, or official gatherings at the convention center, how do the current sound echo, lighting quality, and room comfort affect the overall experience and dignity of the event?"*
* **Waray:** *"Kon naatender ka hin mga kultural nga pasundayag, asembliya, o opisyal nga okasyon ha convention center, paunan-o nakaka-apekto an lanog san tunog, kapawa san suga, ug ka-komportable san lugar ha kadungganan ug katahum san okasyon?"*
* **Interviewer Probes:**
  - Does harsh room echo make it difficult to appreciate speeches, music, or theatrical performances?
  - Does the current visual ambiance feel generic or impersonal like a plain gym, rather than a distinguished civic venue?

---

### Core Question 2 (Authentic Samar Banig & Calbayog Waterfall Representation — Module 4 Q9)
* **English:** *"What are your thoughts on featuring Samar cultural heritage (such as Basey banig weaving geometry and tikog grass craftsmanship) alongside Calbayog natural heritage (such as cascading waterfall motifs) within the interior finishes?"*
* **Waray:** *"Ano an imo panlantaw ha pag-upod san aton kultural nga kabilin (sugad san paglalag san banig nga Basey ug tikog) ug natural nga kabilin (mga busay san Calbayog) ha mga kuta, acoustic wall panels, ug ceiling san hall?"*
* **Dedicated Cultural Probes:**
  - *Authenticity vs. Abstraction:* Do you favor modern geometric abstractions of traditional weaves (*Lara* / *Samat* patterns) over literal, copy-paste depictions?
  - *Tactile Warmth:* How would incorporating natural *tikog* reed textures into acoustic wall surfaces make visitors feel welcomed?
  - *City of Waterfalls:* How can Calbayog's waterfall identity (e.g., Bangon, Tarangban, Mapaso) be represented gracefully in suspended ceiling acoustic elements?

---

### Core Question 3 (Balancing Cultural Pride with Event Theme Versatility — Module 4 Q10)
* **English:** *"How can we express authentic local heritage inside the hall in a way that feels dignified and prominent, yet versatile enough to host diverse event themes—such as modern medical conventions, private wedding banquets, or corporate trade fairs—without visual clashing?"*
* **Waray:** *"Paunan-o naton maipapakita an aton kabilin ha sulod san hall nga magpabilin nga dungganon ug pormal, pero nababagay gihapon ha iba-iba nga okasyon—sugad san medikal nga konperensya, kasal, o trade fair—nga diri nakikig-bunggo ha tema san okasyon?"*
* **Dedicated Cultural Probes:**
  - *Subtlety & Color:* Would neutral timber finishes, warm earth tones, and acoustic fabric textures be better suited than loud, highly saturated colors?
  - *Sense of Place:* When out-of-town delegates or tourists enter the convention center, what immediate feeling or story should the interior convey about Samar and Calbayog?

---

### Core Question 4 (Material Integrity, Craftsmanship, & Longevity — Module 4 Q11)
* **English:** *"From a cultural and civic standpoint, what kinds of durable, sustainable materials and local craftsmanship should be prioritized so that the convention center stays beautiful and proudly represents the community for 10 to 15 years?"*
* **Waray:** *"Para ha imo, ano nga mga lig-on, makauupay ha kalikupan nga materyales ug lokal nga produkto an angay naton gamiton para an convention center magpabilin nga matahum ug magsilbing bugal san Calbayog ha madugay nga katuigan?"*
* **Dedicated Cultural Probes:**
  - *Local Artisan Engagement:* Would incorporating composite acoustic panels made with local *tikog* or engineered timber support local livelihood and craft awareness?
  - *Civic Longevity:* How can we ensure the design feels timeless rather than a passing trend that looks outdated in a few years?

---

### Core Question 5 (Venue Utilization & Civic-Cultural Programming Dynamics — Module 4 Q12)
* **English:** *"In your experience, what makes organizers choose—or avoid—the Convention Center for cultural events, art exhibits, or community gatherings instead of venues like the Handumanan Museum or the Sports Center?"*
* **Waray:** *"Ha imo eksperyensya, kay-ano nga ginpipili (o diri ginpipili) an Convention Center para ha mga cultural events, art exhibits, o mga pagtitirok kompara ha Handumanan Museum o Sports Center?"*
* **Dedicated Cultural & Operational Probes:**
  - *Spatial & Lighting Capabilities:* For art displays or cultural showcases (like the 2024 *Artists Kolektib*), what temporary display panels, lighting, or staging do organizers need that the convention center currently lacks?
  - *Venue Selection:* How can the convention center best complement—rather than compete with—the Sports Center (for large sports/festival showdowns) and the Handumanan Museum (for permanent art collections)?
