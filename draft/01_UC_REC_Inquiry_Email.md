1. **Find the UC-REC Contact Details**: Obtain the official email address of the UC-REC Secretariat (e.g., from the University of Cebu Research & Innovation Office or campus contact page).
2. **Replace Bracketed Placeholders**: Fill in or replace all items enclosed in square brackets `[ ... ]` before sending.
3. **Keep Your Adviser in CC**: Always keep your thesis adviser (`IDr. Kirah Angeline Lampinez, PIID`) copied in the email thread to establish academic credibility and institutional transparency.
4. **Choose Your Preferred Version**:

## Reference Checklist: Standard Items Needed for REC Protocol Submission

While awaiting UC-REC's specific templates, keep the following existing files from your thesis directory ready for conversion into their prescribed format:

- [ ] **Working Manuscript / Research Protocol Summary**: Chapters 1–3 (`02_chapter1.md`, `20_RRL.md`, `30_Research Design.md`).
- [ ] **Data Collection Instruments**: Survey questionnaires and interview guides (`37_Survey&Interview.md`).
- [ ] **Participant Informed Consent Forms**: Participant consent sheets (`38_InformedConsent.md`, `39_InterviewInformedConsent.md`, `40_ObservationInformedConsent.md`).
- [ ] **Endorsement Letter**: Formal letter signed by your adviser (`IDr. Kirah Angeline Lampinez, PIID`) and/or Department Chair endorsing your protocol for external review.
- [ ] **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**: Brief academic CV of the researcher and thesis adviser.
- [ ] **Site Permission / Venue Authorization**: Official communication or permission letter to conduct the study at the Calbayog City Convention Center.
