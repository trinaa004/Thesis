## Executive Overview & Methodological Role

To ensure empirical validity and safeguard against the critique that interior designs are purely hypothetical or decorative, this document provides the complete **12-Slide Evaluative Presentation Deck** and **Operational Scenarios Guide** for the Phase 3 Focus Group Discussion (FGD).

Under **Applied Design Research** (Groat & Wang, 2013; Zeisel, 2006), stakeholder evaluation cannot occur in an abstract vacuum. In this session, venue stakeholders"comprising **Professional Event Planners (129=3-4$)**, **CCCC Facility & Custodial Managers (129=1-2$)**, **Live Audio-Visual / Sound Engineers (129=1-2$)**, **Multi-Event Attendees (129=3-4$)**, and **City Arts & Culture Officers (CACO, 129=1$)**"are presented with **direct side-by-side comparative stimuli**:
1. **Option A (Conventional Status Quo Baseline):** Grounded directly in the facility's 12-year longitudinal utilization ledger (129 = 129$ events, 2014"2026), documented field bottlenecks, unbaffled specular porcelain tile reverberation ({60} = 2.53\text{ s}$), and actual on-site photographic evidence.
2. **Option B (Proposed Modular Interior Framework):** The non-structural interior overlay proposing mobile reconfigurable FF&E, Basey *tikog* acoustic wall panels (NRC $\ge 0.75$), cascading waterfall ceiling baffles (NRC $\ge 0.85$), overhead track-and-rigging utility grids, and BP 344 accessible circulation paths.
