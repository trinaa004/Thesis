## Executive Overview & Methodological Role

To ensure empirical validity and safeguard against the critique that interior designs are purely hypothetical or decorative, this document provides the complete **24-Slide Evaluative Presentation Deck** and **Operational Scenarios Guide** (encompassing all 4 Master Typologies, including the empirically grounded Compound Multi-Use Hybrid Typology) for the Phase 3 Focus Group Discussion (FGD).

Under **Applied Design Research** (Groat & Wang, 2013; Zeisel, 2006), stakeholder evaluation cannot occur in an abstract vacuum. In this session, venue stakeholders—comprising **Professional Event Planners (n = 3–4)**, **CCCC Facility & Custodial Managers (n = 1–2)**, **Live Audio-Visual / Sound Engineers (n = 1–2)**, **Multi-Event Attendees (n = 3–4)**, and **City Arts & Culture Officers (CACO, n = 1–2)**—are presented with **direct side-by-side comparative stimuli**:
1. **Option A (Conventional Status Quo Baseline):** Grounded directly in the facility's 12-year longitudinal utilization ledger (N = 127 events, 2014–2026), documented field bottlenecks, unbaffled specular porcelain tile reverberation ($RT_{60} = 2.53$ s), and actual on-site photographic evidence across representative event types.
2. **Option B (Proposed Modular Interior Framework):** The non-structural interior overlay proposing mobile reconfigurable FF&E, Basey *tikog* acoustic wall panels (NRC ≥ 0.75), cascading waterfall ceiling baffles (NRC ≥ 0.85), overhead track-and-rigging utility grids, and BP 344 accessible circulation paths.
