DEFINITION OF TERMS

Future-Proof
The strategic planning and creation of built environments that remain relevant, functional, and adaptable in the face of evolving needs and conditions.
Local Identity
It is rooted in the lived experience of place, where meaning is created through emotional, cultural, and social connections (Relph, 1976).
Modular Design
A design strategy and common trend in convention center designs that involves either the combination or the separation of areas to cater to different kinds of events (Barr, 2023)
Culturally Responsive Design


An approach that integrates the values, traditions, and spatial practices of a particular culture into the built environment, ensuring that architecture resonates with its users' identities and ways of life (Krstić, Filipović, & Ristić Trajković, 2024).


Typology
Involves examining and categorizing buildings and spatial forms according to shared features or structural patterns, which can be used as guiding models in architectural design (Moneo, 1978).


Adaptive Design
Allows architecture to change and develop over time, offering flexibility to accommodate unexpected modifications and various layouts, which contributes to its resilience and sustainability (Kronenburg,2007).
