## 2. Thesis Executive Context & Core Parameters

- **Official Working Title**:  
  *An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Local Samar Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability*
- **Degree Program**: Bachelor of Science in Interior Design
- **Research Locale & Site Specifications**:
  - **Venue**: Calbayog City Convention Center (Main Convention Hall & Foyer)
  - **Location**: Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar, Philippines
  - **Historical Note**: Renovated on December 14, 2009 (originally a municipal bus station)
  - **Footprint Area**: **840 square meters (711 sqm Main Hall + 129 sqm Foyer)**
  - **Maximum Occupancy**: Up to 500 occupants (Plenary assembly in Main Hall)
- **Primary Operational Bottlenecks**:
  - Rigid, static interior envelope causing prolonged setup/teardown turnaround times.
  - Poor acoustic control and lack of flexible acoustic isolation/baffling.
  - Generic aesthetic envelope lacking localized cultural identity and sense of place.
- **Core Design Strategy**:
  - Non-structural interior overlay comprising modular layout typologies, flexible FF&E, mobile space dividers, overhead track/rigging systems, and localized surface pattern treatments.

## 4. Methodological Summary & Sampling Matrix

- **Framework**: Applied Design Research (Convergent Parallel Mixed-Methods)
- **Methodology**: Convergent Parallel Mixed-Methods Design
- **Respondent Sampling Matrix**:
  - **Group 1: Event Planners & Venue Operators** ($n = 15–25$)
    - *Method*: Purposive Expert Sampling
    - *Focus*: Setup/teardown logistics, turnaround bottlenecks, FF&E mobility, ceiling rigging needs.
  - **Group 2: General Public & Event Attendees** ($n = 100–120$)
    - *Method*: Purposive Quota Sampling (Slovin's Formula, 95% Confidence Level, $e = 0.08–0.09$)
    - *Focus*: Interior comfort, circulation, sightlines, acoustics, wayfinding, cultural ambiance.
  - **Qualitative Key Informants** ($n = 8–12$)
    - *Method*: Semi-structured in-depth interviews (Thematic saturation via Braun & Clarke, 2006).
  - **Total Diagnostic Sample Size ($N$)**: **115 to 145 respondents**
  - **Post-Design Validation Focus Group Discussion (FGD) Panel** ($n = 7–10$)
    - *Method*: Purposive Nested Sub-sampling from Phase 1 respondents (3–4 Event Planners, 1–2 CCC Operations Staff, 2–3 Event Attendees, 1 Cultural Stakeholder).
    - *Focus*: Stress-testing 2D plans and 3D visual models via silent rubric scoring and moderated thematic discussion (Tremblay et al., 2010; Zeisel, 2006; Krueger & Casey, 2014).

## 6. Cultural Design Vocabulary (Samar-Calbayog Synthesis)

- **Regional Samar Heritage**:
  - Basey *banig* reed mat weaving linework (Samat & Lara weaves).
  - *Tikog* grass (*Fimbristylis umbellaris*) natural textures integrated into acoustic wall panels.
  - Traditional Waray geometric motifs.
- **Local Calbayog Identity**:
  - Cascading fluid linework celebrating Calbayog as the "City of Waterfalls" (e.g., Tarangban, Bangon, Mapaso falls).
  - Non-structural acoustic ceiling baffles imitating waterfall fluid mechanics.
