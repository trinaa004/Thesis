## 2. Thesis Executive Context & Core Parameters

- **Official Finalized Thesis Title**:  
  *SPATIAL UTILIZATION ANALYSIS AND EVIDENCE-BASED INTERIOR DESIGN INTERVENTIONS FOR THE CALBAYOG CULTURAL CONVENTION CENTER: OPTIMIZING SPATIAL ADAPTABILITY AND SAMAR HERITAGE INTEGRATION FOR FLEXIBLE EVENT ENVIRONMENTS, HINAY-LA ST., BRGY. CAPOOCAN, CALBAYOG CITY, SAMAR*
- **Degree Program**: Bachelor of Science in Interior Design
- **Research Locale & Site Specifications**:
  - **Venue**: Calbayog Cultural Convention Center (locally referenced as the Calbayog City Convention Center)
  - **Location**: Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar, Philippines
  - **Statutory Mandate**: Officially chartered by the City Government of Calbayog to serve as a vital hub for promoting culture, arts, tourism, performances, art displays, cultural training, and community assemblies.
  - **Historical Note**: Renovated on December 14, 2009 (originally a municipal central bus station)
  - **Footprint Area**: **840 square meters (711 sqm Main Hall + 129 sqm Lobby)**
  - **Maximum Occupancy**: Up to 500 occupants (Plenary assembly in Main Hall)
- **Venue Ecosystem Context**:
  - **Calbayog City Sports Center**: Mega-capacity arena for mass athletics and the outdoor street-dancing competitions of the Hadang Festival.
  - **Handumanan Museum / Arts Gallery**: Municipal facility dedicated to permanent art exhibits and historical collections.
  - **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center**: Intimate, air-conditioned, affordable 840 sqm civic venue for conferences, seminars, social banquets, and curated indoor cultural presentations/convocations.
- **Primary Operational Bottlenecks**:
  - Rigid, static interior envelope causing prolonged setup/teardown turnaround times.
  - Poor acoustic control and lack of flexible acoustic isolation/baffling.
  - Generic aesthetic envelope lacking localized cultural identity and sense of place.
- **Core Design Strategy (The Three Master Modules)**:
  - **Module 1 (Spatial Modularity & Layouts):** Scalable layout template suite across 4 occupancy tiers (50–500 pax: Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition), featuring a **venue-provided demountable modular staging system** (interlocking 1.22 × 2.44 m risers, adjustable 300/600/900 mm heights, BP 344 ramp) replacing the legacy fixed concrete stage—enabling on-demand stage assembly for convocations or complete stage removal ("Zero-Stage Flat-Floor Mode") to reclaim 52.5 sqm of continuous floor area for expos and banquets; enforcing 900 mm seat pitch, 1800 mm service lanes, an absolute 2000 mm door clearance buffer, BP 344 wheelchair bays, and standardized dimensioned layout templates calibrated to structural column gridlines.
  - **Module 2 (Samar-Calbayog Cultural Acoustic Overlays & Proposed Finishes):** Commercial finishes subtly inspired by local heritage—Stage walls: Wallcrown Atmosphere Digest wallpaper (UW1062) in light beige (dried-tikog pattern); Columns: White MCM banig-weave flexible cladding (base) + Oak-painted Flexiflute Primed panels (upper shaft); Grid Line B: Solid acoustic separation wall (staggered light beige fabric acoustic panels with cove lighting inside; Hadang Festival rooster CNC carved mural in 129 sqm Lobby); Full-Height Wand-Drawn beige acoustic drapery (+4,685 mm to floor); suspended waterfall acoustic ceiling baffles (NRC ≥ 0.85); and A-xet acoustic LVT flooring in BIANCO SNOW TERRAZZO.
  - **Module 3 (Overhead Utility, Dropped Soffit, & Smart Lighting):** Perimeter dropped ceiling soffit at +4,685 mm AFFL with inward 24V linear LED cove lighting washing waterfall baffles, downward concealed dual-service pocket (150 × 180 mm) housing Channel A (drapery track) and Channel B (continuous Unistrut P1000 swagging rail eliminating 100% of DIY wall drilling), central motorized hoists (reclaiming 40 guest seats), 3-circuit DALI/DMX dimmable tracks, and illuminated 90-minute battery-backed Fire Exit signage (RA 9514).
