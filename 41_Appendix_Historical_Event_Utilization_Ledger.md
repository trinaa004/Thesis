## 1. Document Overview & Methodological Grounding

This appendix compiles the complete, chronological venue utilization inventory for the **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center** (locally referenced as the *Calbayog City Convention Center*), situated in Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar. Serving as the primary empirical baseline for the study's **Applied Design Research** methodology, this ledger directly corroborates the research findings presented in Chapter 4 (Sections 4.1 to 4.4).

### Methodological Purpose of the Ledger:
1. **Verification of Longitudinal Civic Significance:** Directly addresses institutional panel and adviser inquiries regarding facility viability by proving that the convention center is an actively utilized, indispensable municipal venue rather than a dormant civic shell.
2. **Empirical Justification of Spatial Typologies:** Mathematically grounds the four proposed interior modular layout typologies (**Plenary/Theatrical**, **Classroom/Training**, **Exhibition/Trade/Interactive**, and **Banquet/Social**) against real-world booking demand.
3. **Operational Turnaround Bottleneck Triangulation:** Identifies severe calendar compression periods (e.g., peak multi-typology turnovers during Charter Day festivities), validating the ergonomic setup delays and labor fatigue reported by professional event planners in Chapter 4.
4. **Cultural & Social Placemaking Validation:** Documents the venue's active hosting of regional cultural convocations (e.g., *Samar-wide Artists Kolektib*), culinary traditions (*Tinapa 101 Cookfest*), and communal milestone banquets (*Kasalang Bayan*, *Hermana Night* 500-pax assembly), justifying culturally responsive interior finishes (Basey *tikog* acoustic wall panels and waterfall ceiling baffles).

### Data Privacy & Anonymization Protocol:
In strict compliance with RA 10173, public and institutional assemblies retain their full official titles, hosting agencies, and press citations. Private citizen banquets (weddings, milestone birthdays, and clan gatherings) are recorded using standardized descriptive academic designations (e.g., *'Private Nuptial Celebration & Wedding Reception'*, *'Wedding Reception Audited via Direct On-Site Observation'*) to safeguard participant privacy while transparently auditing real-world spatial density, circulation demands, and furniture layout loads.

### Occupant Scale Estimation & Seating Audit Protocol:
Because the Calbayog Cultural Convention Center operates as a civic municipal facility without electronic turnstile or RFID ticketing gates, historical occupant numbers are audited and reconstructed through a multi-tiered secondary data estimation procedure:
1. **Level 1 (Direct Published Metrics):** Official delegate, attendee, and participant numbers extracted directly from verified municipal press releases, LGU Public Information Office (PIO) bulletins, Department of Education (DepEd) memoranda, and regional media reports (e.g., 295 registered artists for the *Artists Kolektib*, 96 couples + families for *Kasalang Bayan*, 53 creative entrepreneurs for *DTI Training*, 13 cooking teams for *Tinapa 101*, and 500 peak delegates for *Hermana Night*).
2. **Level 2 (Photographic Seating Array Audits):** For events without an explicit published attendee count in the text, archival high-resolution photographs from the City Government of Calbayog and Calbayog Journal archives were systematically audited following physical trace observation principles (Zeisel, 2006). The physical furniture arrays—counting rows of Monobloc chairs (Nrows × Nchairs) and round banquet tables—were enumerated on the 711 sqm Main Hall floor plate.
3. **Level 3 (Statutory Architectural Boundaries & Stratified Tiering):** Physical capacity limits are strictly governed by Section 1004 of the National Building Code of the Philippines (PD 1096) and Section 10.2.8.1 of the Fire Code of the Philippines (RA 9514), where the 711 sqm Main Hall provides an engineered maximum threshold of 500 occupants for unconcentrated plenary seating (0.65 m²/person) and 250 to 300 occupants for banquet dining (1.40 m²/person). Rather than fabricating unverified exact single-digit headcounts, historical occupant scales are categorized into four standardized operational capacity brackets:
   - **Tier 1 (Small / Compact Assembly & Workshops):** 50–120 occupants
   - **Tier 2 (Medium Civic / Workshop / Banquets):** 150–250 occupants
   - **Tier 3 (Large Civic Assembly / Social Banquets):** 250–350 occupants
   - **Tier 4 (Peak Civic Convocations):** 400–500 occupants
4. **Empirical Calibration via Live Event Shadowing:** These secondary archival seating models were directly calibrated and cross-validated against empirical headcounts, furniture layouts, and turnaround times recorded during the researcher's on-site physical shadowing of five live contemporary events (n = 5: *TESDA Graduation*, *Samar Investment Forum*, *Private Wedding Reception*, *PAGPASAMWAK Caravan*, and *Stakeholders' Gala Night*) and confirmed through qualitative interviews with veteran venue booking coordinators (n = 4).

## 4. Key Archival Audit Findings & Triangulation Notes

1. **Structural Durability and Disaster Resilience:** Over the 12-year audit timeframe, the convention center's primary structural envelope (reinforced concrete and structural steel portal frame) successfully endured severe weather events, including Super Typhoon Yolanda (Haiyan, 2013), Typhoon Ruby (Hagupit, 2014), and Typhoon Tisoy (Kammuri, 2019). During crisis periods, the facility served as the city's central emergency relief repacking and distribution operations hub (Item #17–18), establishing its critical civic value to Calbayog City.
2. **Verification of Maximum 500-Occupant Assembly Capacity:** Photographic and organizational records from the *Knights of Columbus Council 3288 Honor Guard: Hermana Night 2026* and mass civic convocations confirm peak occupant loads reaching the full 500-occupant capacity limit of the 711 sqm Main Hall. This verifies the importance of pre-calculating life-safety egress widths under PD 1096 and RA 9514.
3. **Severe Calendar Compression During Annual Festivities:** The ledger reveals that during recurring municipal milestones (notably the week-long Calbayog Charter Day celebrations every October), the 840 sqm interior envelope undergoes back-to-back reconfigurations across highly contrasting typologies (e.g., Job Fair Exhibition -> Drug-Free Symposia Plenary -> Kasalang Bayan Banquet -> Stakeholders' Gala Night -> Tinapa Cookfest). The lack of standardized modular layout protocols and mobile FF&E forces venue custodial crews into exhausting trial-and-error setups lasting 4 to 8+ hours.
4. **Direct Field Observation Validation:** Entry #83 (*Wedding Reception & Banquet, March 18, 2026*) was audited in-person by the researcher. This empirical observation documented real-world banquet table configurations, wait-staff movement patterns, and severe acoustic reverberation across unbaffled perimeter walls, directly informing the interior material specifications and acoustic panel treatments formulated in Phase 2.
