## 2. Quick Typology Reference Guide for Categorization

When categorizing newly added events, refer to the standard thesis typologies:

1. **Plenary / Theatrical**:
   * *Typical Events*: Graduations, opening ceremonies, state of the city addresses, pageants, theatrical cultural performances, general assemblies.
   * *Spatial Setup*: Forward-facing audience seating (rows), stage focus, central/side egress corridors, highest occupant density (up to 500 pax).
2. **Classroom / Training**:
   * *Typical Events*: Departmental capacity building, seminars, workshops, LTO driving courses, DepEd trainings, quiz bees.
   * *Spatial Setup*: Linear writing tables with chairs facing presentation screens, facilitator aisles, power cabling for laptops/projectors.
3. **Banquet / Social**:
   * *Typical Events*: Weddings (*Kasalang Bayan* or private nuptials), milestone birthdays, clan reunions (*Tribe of Mancol*), victory galas, testimonial dinners.
   * *Spatial Setup*: 10-person round banquet tables (3.2–3.5 m footprint), catering buffet lines, wide service lanes (1.6–1.8 m), dance floor or head table.
4. **Exhibition / Trade / Interactive**:
   * *Typical Events*: Trade fairs, produce congresses, medical missions, *Tinapa 101* culinary cookfests, bloodletting drives, job fairs.
   * *Spatial Setup*: Perimeter display booths, interactive demonstration counters, wide two-way pedestrian circulation aisles (1.5–2.4 m).
