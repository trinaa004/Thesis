## 3. Verified Baseline Facts: What to Present to Your Adviser

You now have complete, verified empirical data to present to your adviser:

### A. Building Profile & Client Ownership
* **Official Name:** **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center** (officially chartered on the City Government of Calbayog portal; locally referenced as the *"Calbayog City Convention Center"* or *"Convention Center"*).
* **Location:** Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Western Samar.
* **Owner & Managing Client:** Local Government Unit (LGU) of Calbayog City (under the City Mayor's Office, City General Services Office, City Tourism Office, and City Arts and Culture Office).
* **Statutory Mandate:** Formally chartered by the LGU as a vital hub for promoting Calbayog City’s rich culture, arts, and tourism, featuring spaces for performances (theatres, pavilions), art displays (galleries, museums), cultural training, community gatherings, and local product showcases.
* **Venue Ecosystem Context (Clear Defense Boundary):**
  - **Calbayog City Sports Center:** High-capacity athletic arena for mass-spectator sports, city-wide assemblies, and the outdoor street-dancing showdowns of the **Hadang Festival (historically and popularly recognized as the Sarakiki-Hadang Festival)**.
  - **Handumanan Museum / Arts Gallery:** Municipal facility dedicated to permanent art exhibits and historical collections.
  - **Calbayog Cultural Convention Center:** Intimate, fully air-conditioned, and affordable 840-square-meter civic convention venue for conferences, seminars, social banquets, and curated indoor cultural presentations/convocations.
* **Historical Origin:** Originally constructed as a municipal central bus station; officially renovated and inaugurated as a civic convention facility on **December 14, 2009** (operating for over **17 years** as the city’s premier indoor assembly hub).
* **Interior Floor Plate:** **840 square meters** (711 sqm Main Plenary Hall + 129 sqm Lobby & Pre-function threshold).
* **Assembly Capacity:** Designed to hold up to **500 occupants** simultaneously.

### B. Natural Disaster Resilience (Typhoons Endured)
Samar is located in the typhoon belt of Eastern Visayas. The convention center’s reinforced concrete and structural steel shell has successfully withstood:
1. **Super Typhoon Yolanda (Haiyan, Nov 2013):** Category 5+ super typhoon that ravaged Eastern Visayas; the convention center's primary structural envelope remained intact and served as an essential operational relief and staging point.
2. **Typhoon Ruby (Hagupit, Dec 2014):** Made direct landfall over Eastern/Western Samar with winds exceeding 215 km/h.
3. **Typhoon Tisoy (Kammuri, Dec 2019):** Caused widespread infrastructure damage across Northern and Western Samar.
4. **COVID-19 Pandemic Logistics Hub (2020–2021):** Served as the central municipal operations hub for receiving, repackaging, and dispatching 4,000 50-kg sacks of rice for 35,000 target families (Appendix A, Items #18–19).
* **Thesis Relevance:** Because the architectural superstructure is proven to be structurally durable and typhoon-resilient, it **justifies a non-structural interior overlay**. There is no need to demolish the building; it simply needs an adaptive interior upgrade.

### C. Civic Significance & High-Profile Recurring Events
The Calbayog Cultural Convention Center is the **only LGU-owned indoor convention hall in the entire city**. Verified notable event types include:
* **Cultural & Arts Convocations:** Landmark **Samar-wide Artists Kolektib** (February 27, 2024, National Arts Month), keynoted by Mayor Raymund Uy and renowned visual artist Raul Isidro with 295 registered artists; Waray Wikipedia Cultural & Language Edit-a-thon (Maharlika Sunrise Festival); and curated indoor cultural showcases and receptions for visiting dignitaries.
* **Government & Legislative Assemblies:** *PAGPASAMWAK Sangguniang Panlungsod Legislative Caravan*, Barangay Population Workers (BPW) General Assembly (addressed by Mayor Raymund Uy), Liga ng mga Barangay regular council meetings, and Sangguniang Panlungsod public hearings on municipal economic ordinances.
* **Economic & Commercial Summits:** *2nd Samar Investment Forum* (premier regional economic summit), PESO/DOLE City Mega Job Fairs, and *Lakbay Palay* agricultural conferences.
* **Regional Educational Summits:** DepEd Calbayog Division Teachers’ Assemblies, Early Childhood Care and Development (ECCD) 174-Teacher Edu Workshops, Regional Academic Conferences, and Inter-School Tourism Quiz Challenges (17–21 high schools).
* **Private & Communal Social Banquets:** Knights of Columbus Council 3288 Hermana Night (500 occupants peak capacity), Tribe of Mancol 26th Annual Easter Clan Reunion, *Kasalang Bayan* civil mass weddings (96 couples + families), Annual Charter Day Stakeholders' Gala Night, and private wedding receptions (including an on-site audit conducted by the researcher on March 18, 2026).
