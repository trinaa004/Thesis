## 1. The 1-Sentence Master Anchor (28 Words)

> *"This study develops a site-specific, non-structural interior design framework that optimizes spatial turnaround, acoustic comfort, and Samar-Calbayog cultural identity for the 840 sqm Calbayog City Convention Center."*

---

## 2. The 3-Sentence Elevator Pitch (Problem → Design Action → Outcome)

> *"The Calbayog City Convention Center faces prolonged setup turnaround delays, acoustic reverberation, and generic aesthetics due to a lack of interior spatial standardization.  
> 
> To solve this, the study proposes a **non-structural interior framework** combining four pre-calculated modular layout typologies (*Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition*), Samar Basey banig and waterfall acoustic overlays, and an overhead ceiling utility track.  
> 
> Without altering the building's permanent structural shell, the framework accelerates setup turnaround for event operators while delivering acoustic clarity, accessibility (BP 344), and cultural place attachment for attendees."*

---

## 3. The 2-Paragraph Executive Synopsis (Comprehensive & Protective)

> **Paragraph 1 (The Operational & Spatial Deficit):**  
> *"Originally adapted from a municipal bus terminal, the Calbayog City Convention Center’s 840-square-meter interior envelope (711 sqm Main Hall + 129 sqm Foyer) operates with severe spatial inefficiencies. Because the facility lacks standardized layout templates and on-site furniture storage, event organizers rely on outsourced FF&E and manual trial-and-error setups. This results in excessive setup turnaround times, circulation congestion, poor acoustic speech intelligibility, and a placeless aesthetic envelope that fails to reflect Calbayog's rich civic and cultural identity."*  
> 
> **Paragraph 2 (The Non-Structural Interior Solution & Value):**  
> *"To resolve these deficits without structural demolition or civil engineering intervention, this study formulates an applied Interior Design Framework and Layout Manual grounded in Open Building Theory and Place Attachment. The framework provides a dual-layer intervention: an **active logistical layer** featuring four modular FF&E typologies and an overhead ceiling track that drastically reduces turnaround times; and a **passive experiential layer** featuring acoustic wall panels and suspended ceiling baffles abstracting Samar Basey banig weaving and Calbayog waterfall motifs to restore acoustic comfort and foster regional pride."*

## 5. Defense Guide: Justifying Baseline Problem Claims (Pre-Survey Grounding)

### A. The Anticipated Panel Question
> **Panelist:** *"You state that the convention center has prolonged setup turnaround delays, acoustic reverberation, and generic aesthetics. Where did you get that information if you haven't conducted your survey yet?"*

### B. The 30-Second Verbal Defense Script (Verbatim Speech)
> *"Thank you for that question. In applied interior design research (Groat & Wang, 2013), problem formulation is established prior to formal survey distribution through **preliminary site reconnaissance, physical architectural trace mapping, and facility operational history**:*
> 
> 1. **On Acoustic Reverberation:** The hall’s physical material envelope consists of hard porcelain tile flooring, parallel perimeter glass, and high ceilings with no absorptive treatment. By the basic laws of architectural acoustics, hard non-porous surfaces inevitably cause long reverberation times and flutter echoes.
> 2. **On Turnaround Delays:** Initial administrative inquiry confirmed that the facility was converted from a 2009 bus station and has **no dedicated on-site furniture storage**, forcing third-party event organizers to haul in loose rental furniture and execute setups using manual trial-and-error measurements.
> 3. **On Generic Aesthetics:** Photographic site inventory reveals standard commercial finishes with no reflection of Samar’s indigenous *banig* weaving heritage or Calbayog’s eco-tourism identity.
> 
> *The purpose of my Chapter 3 survey and interview instruments (**Objective 1 / SPOP Q1**) is not to invent these problems, but to **empirically measure, quantify, and statistically validate** their exact severity from the perspective of 115 to 145 actual users and event operators."*
