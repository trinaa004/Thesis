# Interior Design Framework Specification & Operational Deliverables Guide

**Official Thesis Working Title:**  
*An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Local Samar Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability*  
**Degree Program:** Bachelor of Science in Interior Design  
**Spatial Envelope:** 840 square meters (711 sqm Main Convention Hall + 129 sqm Foyer / Pre-function Area)  
**Location:** Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar, Philippines  
**Structural Scope Boundary:** Strictly non-structural interior infill overlay (Zero alterations to columns, facades, trusses, or exterior envelope)

---

## 1. The Master Anchor Definition

> *"A systematic, site-specific operational guide and layout manual comprising pre-calculated modular FF&E floor typologies (Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition), anthropometric clearance standards, non-structural cultural and natural heritage acoustic surface treatments, and overhead ceiling track interfaces designed to guide the rapid spatial reconfiguration and modernization of the 840 sqm Main Hall and Foyer of the Calbayog City Convention Center without structural alteration."*

### Module 1: Pre-Calculated Modular Layout Typologies (Floor Plan Manual)
*Standardized spatial configurations tailored specifically to the 711 sqm Main Hall and 129 sqm Foyer, providing ready-to-execute floor plans that eliminate on-site spatial confusion.*

1. **Plenary / Theater Typology (High-Density Assembly — Up to 500 Occupants):**
   - *Seating Pitch:* Minimum 840 mm to 900 mm back-to-back row spacing.
   - *Aisle Corridors:* 1000 mm side aisles and 1200 mm central arterial aisles ensuring unimpeded life-safety egress (PD 1096 Section 1207(t); RA 9514).
   - *Sightline Verification:* Staggered seating offset providing an unobstructed sightline clearance ($C \ge 60\text{ mm}$) to the elevated stage.
   - *BP 344 Wheelchair Stalls:* Dedicated, integrated $760\text{ mm} \times 1220\text{ mm}$ wheelchair stations distributed within the seating floor (not isolated at the back).

2. **Classroom / Seminar Typology (LGU Workshops & Educational Summits):**
   - *Station Ergonomics:* 740–760 mm table height; 450–500 mm chair clearance behind tables; 400–450 mm service passage.
   - *Density Factor:* Calibrated to the NBCP Table XII.1 standard of $1.80\text{ m}^2\text{/person}$.
   - *Wire Management:* Modesty panels with integrated wire drop raceways connecting to overhead ceiling power drops.

3. **Banquet / Gala Typology (Civic Dining, Weddings & Social Celebrations):**
   - *Cluster Footprint:* 10-person round tables ($\varnothing 1800\text{ mm}$) within a total occupied footprint of 3200–3500 mm including seated guests.
   - *Service Lanes:* Dedicated $1620\text{ mm}$ to $1800\text{ mm}$ clear lanes between outer chair edges to allow two-way catering cart circulation without bumping seated guests.
   - *Density Factor:* Calibrated to NBCP Table XII.1 less-concentrated assembly ($1.40\text{ m}^2\text{/person}$).

4. **Exhibition / Trade Fair Typology (Regional Ecotourism & Product Fairs):**
   - *Booth Modules:* Repeatable $2000 \times 2000\text{ mm}$ or $3000 \times 3000\text{ mm}$ shell-scheme display modules.
   - *Circulation Arteries:* 1500 mm secondary passing corridors; 2400 mm main central avenue accommodating heavy two-way crowd movement.
   - *Travel Distances:* Direct, unobstructed egress routes keeping travel to perimeter exit doors strictly under 45 meters (RA 9514).

5. **Foyer / Pre-Function Sub-System (129 sqm):**
   - Dedicated registration and reception counter module with queuing stanchion paths.
   - Pre-function socializing/cocktail standing nodes ($0.28\text{ m}^2\text{/person}$ waiting allowance).
   - Universal PWD step-free staging zone directly connecting to the Main Hall portals.
   - **Interior Ingress/Egress Cargo Staging Buffer (1800–2400 mm):** A dedicated, unobstructed maneuvering corridor directly inside the main entrance to absorb equipment load-in/load-out without blocking foyer circulation.
   - **Interior Surface Impact Protection:** High-durability wall base and corner guards to protect perimeter interior finishes from cart and dolly collisions during event setup/teardown.

### Module 2: Anthropometric Clearance Standards & Dimensional Ruleset (Eliminating Trial-and-Error)
*The mathematical and spatial foundation of the framework. It eliminates setup delays not by requiring specialized mechanical furniture, but by standardizing the dimensions of typical event furniture and establishing non-negotiable clearance rules so the utility crew never has to guess or rearrange.*

- **Standard Dimensions of Typical Event Furniture:**
  - *Standard Assembly Chair:* $450\text{ mm (width)} \times 450\text{ mm (depth)} \times 800\text{–}850\text{ mm (height)}$.
  - *Standard 10-Person Banquet Table:* $\varnothing 1800\text{ mm}$ ($6\text{ ft}$) circular tabletop.
  - *Standard Seminar / Training Table:* $450\text{ mm} \times 1800\text{ mm}$ (or $600\text{ mm} \times 1800\text{ mm}$) seating 3 participants per unit.
  - *Standard Exhibition Shell Module:* $2000\text{ mm} \times 2000\text{ mm}$ and $3000\text{ mm} \times 3000\text{ mm}$ display footprints.
- **Pre-Calculated Anthropometric Clearance Buffers (Panero & Zelnik; Neufert; NBCP):**
  - *Chair Push-Back Clearance:* Exactly **$450\text{ mm} \text{ to } 500\text{ mm}$** behind banquet and classroom tables, allowing occupants to push back chairs and stand comfortably without colliding with adjacent stations.
  - *Two-Way Catering Service Corridors:* Exactly **$1620\text{ mm} \text{ to } 1800\text{ mm}$** between the perimeter chair edges of adjacent banquet clusters, allowing waiters carrying service trays or food carts to pass freely.
  - *Assembly Row Pitch & Passage:* Exactly **$900\text{ mm}$** row spacing back-to-back, maintaining an unobstructed **$305\text{ mm}$** clear lateral passage lane per NBCP Section 1207(t) when chairs are occupied.
  - *Egress Corridors & Perimeter Setbacks:* Minimum **$1200\text{ mm}$** clear buffer from all perimeter walls and primary exit doors, guaranteeing that furniture placement never encroaches upon required fire exit paths (RA 9514).
- **The "First-Time Accuracy" Protocol:**
  - By providing exact, dimensioned spatial offsets (e.g., *"Plenary Row 1 begins exactly 3.50 meters from the stage front; central aisle is centered on the foyer double doors"*), the crew sets up furniture accurately on the very first pass.
  - Eliminates the 3 to 4 hours typically wasted on eyeballing, discovering that tables block exits, dragging furniture across the hall, and re-spacing rows.

### Module 3: Cultural Acoustic Surface Treatments & IEQ Overlays (Sensory Placemaking)
*Remediates existing acoustic reverberation (flutter echo from ceramic tile flooring and glass) while embedding regional Samar and Calbayog identity.*

- **Perimeter Acoustic Wall Absorption Panels:**
  - Non-structural wall-mounted composite panels (achieving an acoustic performance of NRC 0.75–0.85).
  - Upholstered in flame-retardant commercial acoustic fabric incorporating abstracted geometric patterns derived from **Basey *banig* weaving (*Samat* and *Lara* weaves)** and natural **tikog grass (*Fimbristylis umbellaris*)** textures.
  - Sub-frame installation using concealed Z-clips to avoid damaging the existing building shell.
- **Suspended Kinetic Waterfall Ceiling Baffles:**
  - Non-structural, curvilinear ceiling sound baffles imitating the fluid dynamics and cascading drop lines of **Calbayog’s famed waterfalls** (Tarangban, Bangon, Mapaso falls).
  - Breaks up large ceiling flutter echoes and diffuses mid-to-high speech frequencies.
- **Built-In Cultural Backdrop for Budget Equity:**
  - Provides a dignified, permanently styled cultural backdrop by default.
  - Local public schools, civic organizations, and grassroots event organizers no longer need to rent temporary backdrops or tape/staple single-use tarpaulins, preventing interior wall deterioration.

---

### Module 4: Overhead Ceiling Utility & Lighting Track Interface (Technical Versatility)
*Provides dynamic technical infrastructure without invasive structural modifications.*

- **Ceiling Utility Suspension Interface:**
  - An overhead grid track system anchored to existing ceiling sub-framing (strictly non-load-bearing relative to primary roof trusses).
  - Incorporates raceways for concealed power drops, AV cable management, and signal routing, eliminating tripping hazards and taped floor wires.
- **Adaptive Multi-Zone Illumination (ASEAN MICE / DOT PAS Compliance):**
  - High-efficacy LED track fixtures capable of delivering controllable **300 to 500 lux** for plenary assemblies and classroom note-taking.
  - Seamless dimming presets to warm ambient levels (100–150 lux) for evening social banquets and cultural presentations.
- **Flexible Rigging & Drapery Attachments:**
  - Ceiling track mounting points for lightweight acoustic perimeter drapery, temporary localized zoning curtains, or thematic presentation banners.

## 6. Panel Defense Script: "What is Your Framework?"

> **Panelist:** *"Candidate, can you explain in plain terms what your 'Interior Design Framework' actually is? How does it specifically reduce turnaround time?"*
> 
> **Candidate Speech (Verbatim):**  
> *"Thank you, members of the panel. My framework is an **applied interior design toolkit and site-specific layout manual** engineered specifically for the 840-square-meter interior envelope of the Calbayog City Convention Center. 
> 
> Setup delays in provincial multi-use halls are rarely caused by the furniture itself; they are caused by **trial-and-error space planning**. Because there are no standardized floor plans, municipal utility crews eyeball the spacing, accidentally block emergency exits, run out of room, and spend hours dragging tables and chairs back and forth to fix mistakes.
> 
> My framework resolves this through **four non-structural physical modules**:
> 
> 1. **First, a Pre-Calculated Layout Manual** providing four standardized floor plans—Plenary for up to 500 occupants, Classroom, Banquet, and Exhibition—all verified against National Building Code (PD 1096 Table XII.1), Fire Code (RA 9514), and BP 344 accessibility clearances.
> 2. **Second, an Anthropometric Clearance & Dimensional Ruleset** that establishes the standard dimensions of typical event furniture and provides non-negotiable clearance buffers (such as 900 mm plenary row pitch, 450 mm chair pull-out depth, and 1620–1800 mm two-way banquet service corridors). This gives the utility crew an exact, foolproof blueprint so they set up the room accurately on the very first pass without guessing or rearranging.
> 3. **Third, Cultural Acoustic Overlays** that solve the hall's echo using wall panels and suspended waterfall ceiling baffles that abstract Samar's Basey *banig* weaving and Calbayog's eco-tourism identity. This gives budget-constrained community events a built-in, dignified backdrop without requiring costly disposable decor.
> 4. **Fourth, an Overhead Utility and Lighting Track** that delivers compliant 300 to 500 lux illumination and concealed cable routing from above, keeping floor paths safe and clear.
> 
> In short, it is **an actionable, non-structural spatial manual** that transforms an aging municipal envelope into a future-proof, culturally grounded MICE facility by replacing guesswork with standardized interior design science."*
