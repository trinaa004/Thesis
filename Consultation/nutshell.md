**Official Working Title:**  
*An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Local Samar Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability*

**Degree Program:** Bachelor of Science in Interior Design  
**Research Locale:** Calbayog City Convention Center (Main Convention Hall)  
**Location:** Brgy. Capoocan, Calbayog City, Samar, Philippines

## 1. The 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> *"This study develops a **site-specific, non-structural interior design framework and layout manual** for the **840-square-meter Main Hall of the Calbayog City Convention Center** in Samar.*
> 
> *By harmonizing **spatial modularity (reconfigurable FF&E)**, **Samar-Calbayog cultural heritage (Basey banig weaving and waterfall motifs)**, and **a flexible overhead ceiling interface**, the study transforms an aging, converted municipal hall into an adaptable, high-efficiency civic venue that accelerates event turnaround times, controls acoustics, and complies with universal accessibility (BP 344)—**all within the venue's existing structural shell without requiring building demolition or structural alteration.**"*

### Pillar Breakdown:

1. **Spatial Modularity & Anthropometric Layouts**:
   - **Plenary Layout**: 900 mm seat pitch, 305 mm clear passage, 60/120 mm sightline C-values.
   - **Classroom Layout**: 740–760 mm desk height, 450–500 mm chair depth, 400–450 mm service aisle.
   - **Banquet Layout**: 3200–3500 mm circular table footprints, 1620–1800 mm service lanes.
   - **Exhibition Layout**: 750–900 mm single-file, 1500–1800 mm passing, 2400 mm crowd corridors.
   - **Universal Accessibility (BP 344 & ADA)**: 1200 mm walkways, 1500 mm turning radiuses, 760 x 1220 mm wheelchair stalls with companion seating.

2. **Cultural Responsiveness & Regional Heritage**:
   - **Samar Basey *Banig* Weaving**: Translating traditional *tikog* grass (*Fimbristylis umbellaris*) weaving patterns into modular acoustic wall panels and perimeter finishes.
   - **Calbayog "City of Waterfalls"**: Abstracting the fluid mechanics of Tarangban, Bangon, and Mapaso falls into suspended curvilinear acoustic ceiling baffles.
   - **Placemaking**: Fostering community belonging and place attachment without imposing rigid, non-rentable decorative clutter.

3. **Future-Proofing & Overhead Ceiling Interface**:
   - Utilizing the upper ceiling volume for **lightweight suspended track rails** carrying modular ambient luminaires, acoustic drapes, and spatial dividers.
   - Frees up the 840 sqm floor plane, eliminating floor cable hazards and cutting setup/teardown turnaround times in half.

### Methodological Foundation of the Turnaround Workflow Model

The Turnaround Workflow Model is not arbitrary—it applies established **Spatial Time-and-Motion Staging Analysis** synthesizing three core academic benchmarks:

1. **Pembegül (2009)** (*Design Parameters in Convention Centers and Meeting Facilities*):  
   Establishes that event space turnaround duration is a mathematical function of transport cycles, velocity, and installation tasks:
   $$\text{Turnaround Duration} = \left(\frac{\text{Transport Distance} \times \text{Trips}}{\text{Crew Velocity}}\right) + \text{Placement \& Rigging Time}$$
2. **Panero & Zelnik (1979) / Neufert (2012)** (*Architectural Standards & Human Dimensions*):  
   Establishes physical material-handling velocities and capacity constraints:
   * *Manual Loose Carry*: 2 chairs per trip at $\approx 0.5\text{ m/s}$ walking velocity (high worker fatigue).
   * *Wheeled Dolly Rolling*: 10 chairs per cart at $\approx 0.9\text{ m/s}$ rolling velocity along 1800 mm clear service aisles.
3. **An, Kim, & Hur (2021)** (*Convention Hall Operational Flexibility Metrics*):  
   Establishes the four standard setup operational phases: (1) Layout planning/measuring, (2) Bulk furniture transport, (3) Perimeter drapery/rigging, and (4) Safety clearance verification.

If the panel asks: *"How did you validate that your design actually speeds up turnaround time?"*

**Defense Script**:
> *"We utilized a **Spatial Time-and-Motion Staging Analysis** based on **Pembegül (2009)** and **Panero & Zelnik (1979)**. 
> 
> By capturing baseline setup durations from our Calbayog survey and interviews, we mapped the logistical transport distance inside the 840 sqm hall. Moving 500 loose chairs by hand requires 250 trips totaling 15,000 meters of walking. By integrating 10-unit wheeled dollies, pre-calculated floor grid templates, and suspended ceiling track drapery, the proposed framework reduces physical travel distance by 80% and slashes turnaround time from **over 6.5 hours down to approximately 1.2 hours**—all while maintaining full BP 344 accessibility compliance."*
