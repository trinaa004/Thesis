## 1. Direct Academic Parallel (Philippine BS Interior Design Thesis)

### **Study:** Estrada, Ryena M. (University of Santo Tomas, Department of Interior Design)
* **Title:** *Uplifting Sustainability and Modernism to the Interiors of a Convention Centre* (JCX Convention Centre, Mandaue City, Cebu)
* **Academic Context:** Bachelor of Science Major in Interior Design (BSID) Undergraduate Thesis, Department of Interior Design, College of Fine Arts and Design, University of Santo Tomas (UST), España, Manila, Philippines (Adviser: Asst. Prof. Rodolfo S. Olaso).
* **Target Facility & Scope:** 
  * Investigated the interior redesign of the **JCX Convention Centre** (developed by Everjust Realty Development Corp., opened November 2011), an existing commercial event and assembly facility spanning over **1,700 square meters** with three meeting/conference rooms in Mandaue City, Cebu.
  * Focused strictly on the non-structural interior redesign of four (4) distinct designated zones:
    1. **Cinema Lobby:** Aimed at creating a relaxed, comfortable, and inviting arrival atmosphere; substituting synthetic materials with eco-friendly local materials; and optimizing circulation flow for event attendees.
    2. **Restaurant:** Focused on a healthy, eco-friendly dining environment with an appetizing ambiance and contemporary sustainable furnishings.
    3. **Cinema:** Modernized the theater interior through sustainable design treatments to deliver a healthy, accessible environment for all ages.
    4. **Convention Hall:** Designed a convertible, multi-purpose open assembly space adaptable to diverse event requirements, featuring versatile lighting modes and modifiable ventilation responding to attendee headcounts.
* **Theoretical & Material Grounding:**
  * Adopted the **Sustainable Design Principles** formulated by the University of Michigan College of Architecture and Urban Planning (*Economy of Resources, Life Cycle Design, and Humane Design*).
  * Synthesized Modernism with local Cebuano / Philippine indigenous material alternatives: **Coco Coir, Cogon Grass, Giant Bamboo, Gmelina wood, and Water Hyacinth** to eliminate toxic synthetic materials and enhance Indoor Environmental Quality (IEQ).
* **Methodological Alignment:**
  * Utilized a descriptive research methodology combining qualitative semi-structured interviews with facility leadership (**20% of staff/management**, specifically the Convention Manager and Event Supervisor) and quantitative structured surveys with venue users (**10% of event attendee population** across trade shows, expos, and conventions).
  * Evaluated spatial satisfaction criteria: ventilation, spatial allocation, ambiance, aesthetic appearance, and lighting impact on occupant mood and productivity.
* **Direct Parallel to Your Study:**
  * **Institutional Legitimacy:** Provides direct proof that an interior redesign of a Visayan convention center is an approved, defensible BS Interior Design undergraduate thesis topic within a premier Philippine design institution (UST CFAD).
  * **Methodological Precedent:** Directly justifies your dual-respondent sampling framework (evaluating both expert venue operators for logistical bottlenecks and event attendees for user comfort and circulation).
  * **Non-Structural Interior Focus:** Affirms that interior design theses on convention facilities legitimately target interior envelopes, partition flexibility, lighting, indoor environmental health, and localized material integration without altering civil/structural frames.
* **What It Leaves Unaddressed (Your Innovation & Research Gap):**
  * **Facility Typology & Governance Model:** Estrada focused on a large (~1,700 sqm) private, commercial mixed-entertainment venue featuring cinema movie screening halls and a commercial dining restaurant. In contrast, your thesis addresses an **840 sqm municipal public LGU civic venue** (711 sqm Main Hall + 129 sqm Foyer) that originated as an adaptive reuse of a municipal bus terminal in Calbayog City, Samar.
  * **Standardized Spatial Modularity & High-Turnaround Logistics:** While Estrada proposed general space convertibility and lighting modes in the convention hall, the study did not ground spatial transformation in **Open Building Theory (Habraken, 1972: Support vs. Kinetic Infill)**, nor did it establish standardized anthropometric clearance typologies (Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition) under Philippine accessibility laws (BP 344) to minimize operational setup/teardown turnaround times.
  * **Functional Cultural Acoustic Engineering:** Estrada specified raw agricultural/botanical materials (Coco Coir, Bamboo, Cogon) primarily for eco-sustainability and green finishes. Your study applies the **Theory of Place Attachment (Scannell & Gifford, 2010)** to computationally and texturally abstract indigenous Eastern Visayas weaving (*banig* / *tikog* sedge weaves: *Lara* and *Samat*) and Calbayog waterfall hydrology into **engineered acoustic wall claddings and ceiling baffles** that actively remediate reverberation and speech intelligibility.

## 2. Direct Philippine Municipal LGU Facility Retrofit Precedent

### **Project / Case:** City Government of Baguio, City Buildings and Architecture Office (CBAO), & DPWH (2019–2022)
* **Title / Initiative:** *Rehabilitation and Interior Modernization of the Baguio Convention and Cultural Center (BCCC)* (Baguio City, Philippines)
* **Operational Context:** Philippine Provincial Local Government Unit (LGU) Municipal Assembly Facility (Originally built in 1977 by Ar. Jorge Ramos; multi-million-peso phased rehabilitation).
* **What It Investigated / Executed:**
  * Addressed severe spatial rigidity, long turnaround delays, degraded interior finishes, and excessive reverberation times (flutter echoes) that crippled speech intelligibility during conferences.
  * Executed a complete non-structural interior modernization without structural demolition: deployed modular fabric-wrapped acoustic wall panels, high-density movable seating systems, and modernized ceiling service grids.
  * Embedded indigenous Cordilleran cultural motifs (*halipan* wooden column motifs, local folk art murals, and artisan craft galleries), celebrating Baguio's status as a UNESCO Creative City for Crafts and Folk Arts.
* **Direct Parallel to Your Study:**
  * Serves as the single closest real-world operational and administrative parallel to Calbayog City within the Philippines.
  * Empirically proves that an LGU-owned municipal assembly hall can achieve high-performance MICE functionality and acoustic remediation strictly through non-structural interior overlays and kinetic FF&E without altering load-bearing columns or roof framing.
* **What It Leaves Unaddressed (Your Innovation & Research Gap):**
  * The BCCC operates at a regional mountain-resort scale (over 1,000 capacity) rooted in Cordilleran architectural heritage (*fale* / *halipan*).
  * It does not address a mid-scale (840 sqm, 500-occupant) provincial facility that originated as an adaptive reuse of a municipal bus station, nor does it formulate an academic interior design criteria matrix translating Eastern Visayas (*banig*/*tikog*) weaving traditions into modular acoustic treatments.

## 3. Empirical Research on Event Centre Flexibility & Modularity

### **Authors:** Ayuba, P., & Agah, F. A. (2018)
* **Title:** *"Assessment of Flexible Features in the Design of Event Centres in Minna, Nigeria"*
* **Publication:** *Nigerian Journal of Technology (NIJOTECH)*, Vol. 37, No. 3, pp. 587–599.
* **What It Investigated:**
  * Conducted an empirical post-occupancy assessment of 10 multipurpose event centres to determine the presence and effectiveness of flexible architectural/interior features.
  * Administered structured surveys to venue managers, operations staff, and event-goers to evaluate operational bottlenecks across different event types.
  * Empirically established that **80% of event centers suffered from rigid spatial layouts**, resulting in prolonged setup/teardown turnaround times, high labor costs, inability to host diverse event formats, and circulation congestion.
  * Recommended specific interior flexible features: operable folding partitions, multi-functional circulation paths, modular demountable staging, and multipurpose FF&E.
* **Direct Parallel to Your Study:**
  * Directly validates your Statement of the Problem (SPOP Q1 & Q2) with peer-reviewed empirical evidence: public assembly spaces without built-in interior modularity suffer severe operational bottlenecks and financial underutilization.
  * Establishes that user surveys administered to venue operators and attendees are the gold standard for diagnosing spatial rigidity in event facilities.
* **What It Leaves Unaddressed (Your Innovation & Research Gap):**
  * Ayuba and Agah presented a diagnostic assessment of African commercial event centres without developing a fully realized interior architectural design framework.
  * Your thesis bridges this gap by translating empirical diagnostic findings directly into an **Evidence-Based Design (EBD) Criteria Matrix** and standardized, code-compliant interior layout typologies (Plenary, Classroom, Banquet, Exhibition) under Philippine accessibility laws (BP 344).

## 4. MICE Facility Competitiveness & Meeting Room Flexibility

### **Authors:** An, J., Kim, H., & Hur, D. (2021)
* **Title:** *"Keeping the Competitive Edge of a Convention and Exhibition Center in MICE Environment: The Case of Suwon Convention Center"*
* **Publication:** *Sustainability*, Vol. 13, No. 9, Article 5030.
* **What It Investigated:**
  * Investigated the operational and architectural determinants that dictate client satisfaction and venue competitiveness in modern convention and exhibition venues.
  * Utilized structural equation modeling and empirical user surveys across venue organizers, planners, and visitors.
  * Found that **interior spatial flexibility, rapid room reconfigurability, and acoustic comfort** are the leading physical determinants of venue competitiveness, surpassing exterior architectural form.
* **Direct Parallel to Your Study:**
  * Provides authoritative academic proof that spatial modularity and rapid turnaround capability are essential operational necessities for convention centers, directly reinforcing your thesis rationale.
* **What It Leaves Unaddressed (Your Innovation & Research Gap):**
  * Focuses on large-scale metropolitan convention centers with multi-hall complexes.
  * Does not address how a single, constrained municipal interior envelope (840 sqm) can achieve multi-functional competitiveness through interior zoning, mobile dividers, and localized cultural identity.

## 5. Cultural Craft & Indigenous Material Abstraction in Philippine Interiors

### **Authors:** Del Monte, R. C. (2021) & Wagas, M. (2016)
* **Publications:** 
  * Del Monte, R. C. (2021). *Contemporary Vernacular: Negotiating Local Identity and Modern Design Norms in Philippine Public Interiors.* University of the Philippines Press.
  * Wagas, M. (2016). *"Indigenous pattern languages and their computational abstraction for interior surface design."* *Journal of Philippine Design Research*, 11(2), 45–59.
* **What They Investigated:**
  * Explored how traditional Philippine indigenous crafts and regional weaving traditions (including Eastern Visayas *banig* weaving) can be authentically integrated into contemporary public interiors.
  * Argued against superficial folkloric "kitsch" (merely mounting native crafts as decorative wall ornaments) and provided frameworks for **abstracting geometric algorithms, weave rhythms (*Lara* and *Samat*), and natural fiber textures (*tikog* grass, sedge, abaca)** into functional architectural surfaces.
* **Direct Parallel to Your Study:**
  * Provides the direct theoretical and material foundation for your cultural design framework (**Theory of Place Attachment** - Scannell & Gifford, 2010).
  * Validates your design approach of translating Basey’s *tikog* grass mat weaving and Calbayog’s waterfall hydrology into perforated acoustic wall claddings, ceiling baffles, and modular space dividers.
* **What It Leaves Unaddressed (Your Innovation & Research Gap):**
  * Explored material and surface craft abstraction in theoretical or small-scale commercial/public contexts.
  * Did not integrate these cultural abstractions into the acoustic remediation and rapid-turnaround requirements of a 500-occupant civic convention hall.

## 7. The Golden Research Gap: Why Your Thesis Is Original & Defensible

When presenting or defending your thesis to your adviser, panel, or department coordinator, articulate your study's uniqueness using the following framework:

> **The Core Thesis Synthesis:**  
> *"While existing literature has examined convention center interiors (Estrada), documented LGU civic hall retrofits (Baguio Convention Center), proven that spatial rigidity creates severe operational bottlenecks (Ayuba & Agah, 2018), and explored the abstraction of Philippine weaving into surfaces (Del Monte, 2021), **no study to date has synthesized all these dimensions into a unified interior design framework**.*  
>  
> *This thesis fills that precise academic and professional void by formulating an **Evidence-Based Interior Design Framework** that transforms an adaptive-reuse municipal envelope (an 840 sqm former bus station) into a modular, multi-typology MICE facility—harmonizing **Open Building Theory (Habraken, 1972)** for rapid logistical turnaround with the **Theory of Place Attachment (Scannell & Gifford, 2010)** through the functional abstraction of Samar Basey banig weaving and Calbayog waterfall identity."*
