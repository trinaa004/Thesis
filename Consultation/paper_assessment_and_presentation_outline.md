# PART 1: EXECUTIVE IDENTITY, TITLE, & DISCIPLINARY BOUNDARIES

### Core Facts & References to Present:
* **Working Title**: *An Interior Design Framework for the Calbayog City Convention Center: Harmonizing Spatial Modularity, Local Samar Heritage, and Future-Proof Event Adaptability* ([00_title.md](file:///C:/Users/Trina/OneDrive/Desktop/thesis%201/New%20Thesis/00_title.md)).
* **Site Context**: 840 sqm single-volume Main Hall, 500-occupant capacity, renovated on Dec 14, 2009 from a bus terminal ([31_Research environment.md](file:///C:/Users/Trina/OneDrive/Desktop/thesis%201/New%20Thesis/31_Research%20environment.md)).
* **Existing Finishes (Site Visit Observations)**: Ceramic tile flooring, perimeter glass walls with old, worn acoustic drapery, and aged fabric ceiling baffles needing non-structural upgrading.
* **Professional Scope Boundary (RA 10350)**: Strictly non-structural interior overlay (reconfigurable FF&E, suspended ceiling track interface, acoustic surface claddings). Zero alterations to load-bearing columns, trusses, or exterior facades ([Consultation/nutshell.md#L112-L119](file:///C:/Users/Trina/OneDrive/Desktop/thesis%201/New%20Thesis/Consultation/nutshell.md#L112-L119)).

> **(Say to Adviser / Panel):**  
> *"Good morning, Ma’am/Sir. Today I am presenting our thesis proposal for the Calbayog City Convention Center’s 840 square meter Main Hall. Our title is anchored on three clear pillars: Spatial Modularity, Local Samar Heritage, and Future-Proof Adaptability.  
> 
> As an Interior Design study under RA 10350, our project maintains a strict non-structural scope boundary. Following Habraken’s Open Building principles, we treat the 2009 building shell, columns, and roof trusses as the fixed 'Support,' while our design intervention focuses 100% on the adaptable 'Infill'—reconfigurable modular furniture, non-structural ceiling track systems, and localized acoustic surface upgrades."*

# PART 3: CHAPTER 2 — THEORETICAL & CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK

### Core Facts & References to Present:
* **Dual Theoretical Pillars** ([21_theoretical_conceptual.md](file:///C:/Users/Trina/OneDrive/Desktop/thesis%201/New%20Thesis/21_theoretical_conceptual.md)):
  1. **Open Building Theory (Habraken, 1972)**: Separates fixed base building (Support) from kinetic, demountable interior fit-out (Infill).
  2. **Theory of Place Attachment & PPP Model (Scannell & Gifford, 2010)**: Connects the Person (attendee/citizen), Process (affective pride & cognition), and Place (spatial/cultural ambiance).

        ▼                                                               ▼

> **(Say to Adviser / Panel):**

Open Building Theory by John Habraken (1972) provides the structural-versus-interior operational logic that empowers event planners to execute rapid setup turnarounds without structural renovation. Simultaneously, the Theory of Place Attachment by Scannell & Gifford (2010) ensures that the interior is not a generic corporate box, but a culturally resonant civic venue that fosters local Waray pride through authentic Samar motifs and tactile materials."*

# PART 6: CHAPTER 3 — PHASE 4: DELIVERABLE VALIDATION & WORKFLOW MODELING

### Core Facts & References to Present:
* **How the Deliverable is Validated** ([30_Research Design.md#L31-L47](file:///C:/Users/Trina/OneDrive/Desktop/thesis%201/New%20Thesis/30_Research%20Design.md#L31-L47)):
  1. **Digital 2D/3D CAD Spatial Simulation**: Verifies 1200 mm wheelchair travel paths, 1500 mm turning radiuses (BP 344), and sightline C-values (60 mm / 120 mm).
  2. **Logistical Turnaround Workflow Modeling**: Time-and-motion simulation based on **Pembegül (2009)** and **Panero & Zelnik (1979)** comparing manual loose chair hauling against 10-chair wheeled dollies along designated 1800 mm staging aisles.
  3. **Expert Practitioner Evaluation Rubric ($n = 3–5$)**: Structured 5-point rating by Licensed Interior Designers, Architects, and Venue Operators across 5 operational criteria.

### The Staging Math to Point Out:
* *Baseline Manual Method*: 500 loose chairs $\div$ 2 per trip = 250 trips $\times$ 60m round trip = **15,000 meters of walking** $\approx$ **5.5 hours** of hauling.
* *Proposed Modular Method*: 500 chairs on 10-unit dollies = 50 trips $\times$ 60m = **3,000 meters of rolling** $\approx$ **55 minutes** for a 4-man crew (**81% total turnaround reduction** from 6.5 hrs to 1.2 hrs).

> **(Say to Adviser / Panel):**  
> *"To ensure our thesis does not stop at an unverified concept, Phase 4 formally validates the deliverable through three methods:  
> 
> First, a digital CAD spatial clearance desk-check that audits 100% compliance with BP 344 accessibility rules and sightline C-values.  
> 
> Second, a Logistical Turnaround Workflow Model grounded in Pembegül (2009) and Panero & Zelnik (1979). Our time-and-motion equations demonstrate that replacing manual chair carrying with 10-unit wheeled dollies and ceiling track drapery reduces material travel distance by 80%, cutting total setup turnaround from over 6.5 hours down to 1.2 hours.  
> 
> Third, the completed layout manual is submitted to a panel of 3 to 5 expert practitioners—including Licensed Interior Designers and Venue Managers—for structured rubric evaluation."*
